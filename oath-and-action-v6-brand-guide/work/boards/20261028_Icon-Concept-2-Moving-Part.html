Moving part

Each subject is built from two to four solid pieces held apart by one constant cut. The red piece is the one that moves: the lid lifting, the top stage rising, the door leaf swinging, the ballot going in, the second banknote changing hands. Charcoal holds the structure around it.

2. The idea and the red

The brand's central idea is the hinge, a promise followed by action. Here every subject has one part that moves, and that part is red. It nests against a Charcoal piece across the cut, so Charcoal draws most of its outline and the subject still reads with the red turned to ink or removed (the second and third drawings in each card). Rule 05.3 governs, as D2-01 words it:

"An icon's red accent counts as signature red when the icon stands alone, as an app tile, an avatar or a single feature icon. In a row or a menu, icons go one color and only the current one keeps its red."

4. Grid and craft rules

GridRound one's 24-unit grid, 2-unit padding, live area 2 to 22. Keylines: circle 20, square 18, portrait 16 by 20, landscape 20 by 16.
PiecesTwo to four solid pieces per icon from the kit: square, rectangle, disc, half disc, quarter ring, stadium and 45 degree triangle.
Cut1.5 units in the display master, 2 px at 24 px, 1 px at 16 px, the same on every side of every piece.
CornersConvex corners take a 1-unit radius in the display master and stay square in the hinted masters. Concave corners stay square. Arrowhead corners take half a unit, an optical correction, since a full unit blunts an acute point.
Counters3 units or more at 24 units. Two knock-outs sit under it and are flagged in section 12: Ballot's oval mark (2.2 units tall) and the cuts that set Bill's text lines and Campaign money's corner marks (1.5 units, the cut itself).
Node3 units across in the display master, with a bite of the node plus the cut around it, Direction A's 6-unit seat. It is ink in Explain, Track and Act and a Newsprint knock-out in Connect, where it falls on red. At 24 px it is a 4 px disc; at 16 px a 2 px square, with the bite widened so the square's corners keep a full cut.
RedOne piece, 12 to 25 percent of the painted area. It is the one piece allowed off the 90 degree grid, turned up to 20 degrees, and it moves no more than 2 units from the gap it left.
WeightInk and red together paint 24 to 32 percent of the field at 96 px, within 15 percent of the set's median.
HintingThe 24 px master is the drawing redrawn with a 2-unit cut and square corners. The 16 px master is the display geometry scaled by 2/3, which turns the 1.5-unit cut into 1 px. Horizontal and vertical edges move to whole pixels, both sides of each cut together. Turned and curved edges stay true vectors. Feedback loop at 24 and 16 px and Testify at 16 px are drawn by hand in whole pixels.

7. In a row: idle and current

Idle icons go one color, and the cut still shows the moving part. The current icon keeps its red with a 1 px Charcoal keyline at 16 and 24 px on Newsprint, since red on Newsprint measures 2.96:1. The keyline stops a pixel short of any Charcoal piece, so at 16 px the 1 px cut stays open. The red piece moves one step further open, 1 px at 24 and 16 px, and the label goes bold. On Charcoal the red needs no keyline (4.49:1). On Prussian the icons are White with no red, and the current one shows by its open piece and its bold label.

15. Decisions for Dustin

  1. Act's full lintel (named approval, IC-04). Options: keep the full lintel, or draw the frame over the opening only. Recommendation: keep it. Round one measured 0.60 against Inter Tight 800 "01" without it and 0.42 with it; this 16 px master scores 0.07.
  2. Topic symbols carry the node only where it is part of the subject. This narrows Y5. In this concept no topic symbol carries the pillar node; Feedback loop's two nodes are its subject. Recommendation: approve.
  3. No line companion. This concept has no line cut, so it drops Y4 if chosen. Recommendation: decide it with the concept, since Overprint and Public sign answer it differently.
  4. Under 48 px a pillar mark always sits with its word. Studio proposal from the creative director's rules. Recommendation: approve; the menu row and the topic list show it.

For the creative director

  1. Connect's bridge is 4.5 units tall where the notes asked for about 3, so the 3-unit knock-out keeps 0.75-unit walls. A 3-unit bar would need a 1.5-unit knock-out, smaller than the other nodes.
  2. Feedback loop stands upright. With the nodes on the horizontal center line, as the notes set, it read as a face at 24 px. It also sits under the weight band at 16.8 percent painted, since a heavier drawing pushed its red past 25 percent.
  3. Housing is redrawn after the notes said to keep it: the 16 px similarity check scored the stage 2 draft 0.68 against Ballot. The narrower body under wider eaves and the centered door bring every pair under 0.60.
  4. Track's red block sits in the badge corner (x 16 to 22, y 2 to 8), attached across the cut to the stair below it; Campaign money's red note crosses it too. Neither stands alone there.
