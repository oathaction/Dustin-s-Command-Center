Field Guide

Dustin’s reference card came from Concept 08, the Loop Field Guide. This direction takes the name at its word: every loop is a specimen card drawn one way, and the catalog is a set of plates where each card is the R8 card at a smaller size. The head of the catalog teaches one card at full size, and a reader then reads all 23 the way a birder reads a guide.

What a first-time reader grasps without a key: boxes are things that change; the line across the top runs forward; the line underneath, or up the left side, brings the last change back to the first box.

Built to the creative director’s brief (work/handoffs/phase-2/20261028_CD-Review-Of-Map-Directions.md, section 4): the notation budget by scale, Rule 05.3, the 13 px floor, Lora Italic only for V7’s read-aloud sentence, constant stroke weight within a view, no truncated label and no row with one card alone. Every page below is shown first as a page at its true composition, then at 390 px and in grayscale, then its spec and what the wall and 2x tests changed.

Who Do They Answer To? · Systems Atlas · Loop guide

Twenty-three loops

Every loop in the atlas, drawn one way. Learn this card, and you can read the other twenty-two.

  1. 1A box is something that can rise or fall Its name is V7’s. On a card, a line under it says what a higher value means.
  2. 2The top line runs forward A change in one box changes the next box along the arrow.
  3. 3The line underneath comes back It carries the last change back to the first box. That return is what makes a loop.
  4. 4The sign says which way + means the two move the same way; − means they move opposite ways. A plus is not good news.
  5. 5The texture says how sure Solid: documented or well supported. Dashed: plausible. Dash-dot: contested. Dotted: a hypothesis.

Least certain step

No single step; every step is well supported. V7, on L39, “it is the weaker step because equity is one motive among several for opposing construction.”

One turn

Over 10 years. V7 gives a loop the delay of its slowest step.
