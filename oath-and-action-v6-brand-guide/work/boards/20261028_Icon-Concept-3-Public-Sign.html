Public sign

Oath & Action's subjects drawn the way public information symbols are drawn: people and objects built from one bar on a 45 and 90 degree grid, plain enough to read across a room. The red marks the verb, the one stroke that shows what happens to the subject.

A proposal for the creative director and Dustin, October 28, 2026. No master in the guide changes. The blind name test on these finished masters is run by the main session and is not on this board yet.

14. Decisions

For Dustin's named approval

  1. The pillar marks change treatment. Direction A's skeletons stay; the solids become bars with round ends and a disc node. Options: approve the bar treatment for this concept, or keep the solids and drop the concept. Recommendation: decide only if Public sign wins, since it changes Y1's solid treatment.
  2. Act keeps its full lintel (round one's IC-04). Options: full lintel, or the frame over the opening only. Recommendation: the full lintel, because without it the doorway reads as the letter n at 24 px.
  3. Explain, if it fails the name test. Its skeleton then goes to Dustin as a question, with the drawings beside the approved one. Options: the hinged lid at 45 degrees (the master), the lid open 90 degrees with a true quarter-circle swing (a study, over the red ceiling), or the approved lifted quarter. Recommendation: the master; the 90 degree lid reads as a tab.
  4. Topic symbols carry a node only where it is part of the subject. This narrows Y5. Recommendation: approve; Ballot, Bill, Testify, Housing and Campaign money carry no node.

For the creative director

  1. Feedback loop's return runs over the top. Note 10 asks for it below. Options: as drawn, or below. Recommendation: over the top, because two nodes over a curve read as a smiling face.
  2. Bill stays over the weight band at 37.9%. Options: keep the wide page, or narrow it into the band and fail similarity with Explain. Recommendation: keep; D1-16 protects the name.
  3. Feedback loop stays under the band at 18.0%. Recommendation: accept; the drawing is two nodes and two paths.
  4. Explain's swing is 45 degrees. Note 1 asks for a lid turned 45 degrees and a quarter-circle swing; one lid cannot do both. Recommendation: keep the 45 degree lid.
  5. App tile at 62 percent. Chosen by eye from 56, 62 and 66 (section 10).
