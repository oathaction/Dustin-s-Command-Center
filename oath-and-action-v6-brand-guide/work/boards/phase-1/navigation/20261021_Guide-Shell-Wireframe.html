05 Color

Choose a surface first, then the text and graphic colors that pass on it.

Wireframe of the chapter template. Token names come from the token schema draft (work/build/tokens/20261021_Token-Schema-Draft.json) and can still change at Gate 1. Gray dashed boxes stand in for specimens.

Rule 05.1 Newsprint leads.

Newsprint covers 60 to 85 percent of every composition. Charcoal carries text and structure. A dark panel is a beat, one per section at most.

Tokens --oa-color-palette-newsprint --oa-color-palette-charcoal --oa-color-palette-prussian --oa-color-palette-white

Why it works

Design judgment. A warm, light ground keeps long reading calm, and a rare dark panel tells the reader a section has turned.

Correct application, placeholder: a report spread at true size, Newsprint about 72 percent.
Failure case, placeholder: a page split between Charcoal and Prussian with no reading surface.

Rule 05.2 Text sits only on pairs that pass.

Small text needs a contrast ratio of 4.5 to 1 against its surface. Large text needs 3 to 1; 06 Type says which sizes count as large. Read each pair's ratio in the Specifications table. Never judge it by eye.

Tokens --oa-color-text-primary-on-newsprint #121212 --oa-color-text-secondary-on-newsprint #4E535B, Deep Stone --oa-color-text-primary-on-prussian #FFFFFF

Nothing copied yet.

Why it works

WCAG 2.2 success criterion 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) sets 4.5 to 1 for text and 3 to 1 for large-scale text, which WCAG defines as 18 point, or 14 point bold. Readers with low vision need that margin, and so does anyone reading a phone outdoors. Stone was V5's secondary text color, and it measures 4.29 to 1 on Newsprint. The token draft gives secondary text on Newsprint a derived value, Deep Stone (#4E535B), at 5.49 to 1.

Rule 05.3 One working red per view.

The wordmark's ampersand and the lockmark's lever are signature red and do not count. Each view (one screen, card, slide or printed page) then carries one working red, in this order of precedence: an error the reader must fix; the step a reader can take now; the one point the headline names, which in a systems figure is where a reform acts. A hinge headline's ampersand uses the view's working red. The hinge divider's ampersand is Charcoal. The Act handle is red only when the Act mark is itself the call to act, as on a social tile. Red never sits on Prussian.

Tokens --oa-color-palette-pillar-red --oa-color-palette-charcoal with --oa-border-width-hairline for the keyline

Why it works

Design judgment. One working red tells the eye where to go, and the order of precedence settles which mark gets it when two compete. Red on Newsprint measures 2.96 to 1, under the 3 to 1 that 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast asks of informative graphics, so an essential red graphic takes a 1 px Charcoal keyline.

Correct application, placeholder: one red bar, the point the headline names, with a Charcoal keyline.
Failure case, placeholder: a red rule, a red button and red values in one view.

Rule 05.4 State color travels with a word or a shape.

Every interface state shows a text label or a change of shape as well as a color. A disabled control stays readable and says what will make it available. State colors are their own set, kept apart from identity color and from 14 Data visualization palettes.

Tokens --oa-color-focus-ring-on-newsprint --oa-border-focus-offset --oa-color-state-selected-band-on-newsprint --oa-border-width-bar --oa-color-state-error-band-on-newsprint --oa-color-text-secondary-on-newsprint

Why it works

WCAG 2.2 1.4.1 Use of Color bars color as the only way to convey a state, and 1.4.11 asks 3 to 1 for the edges and focus indicators that identify a control. In V5 the Active and Ready lever states differed by hue alone, at 1.14 to 1 between the two.
