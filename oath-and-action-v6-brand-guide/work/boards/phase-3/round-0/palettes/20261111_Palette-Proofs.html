Data palettes, measured

Every data palette our charts use, drawn on each ground it may sit on, with its contrast, its grayscale and three simulations of color-vision deficiency. Two colors are new: the Prussian steps that let the diverging scale work in the dark theme. Every other proposed token reuses a value the tokens already hold.

Diverging pair B

Values above and below a midpoint: Prussian on one side and Verdigris on the other, through a midpoint tinted toward the ground (DR-54). The dark theme needs the scale on Charcoal (DR-204), and the release holds Newsprint steps only.

On Charcoal the Verdigris half reuses the sequential scale's steps 3 and 4. The Prussian half takes two new mixes, Prussian 56 and 29 percent toward Newsprint, picked so each Prussian step matches its Verdigris partner in lightness (0.633 with 0.634, and 0.478 with 0.476). The midpoint reuses the Charcoal band tint.

Matched ends look alike in gray and to a reader with deuteranopia (the ends sit 0.028 apart there), which is why the boxed arrow or a printed sign carries the direction in every cell, on both grounds.

04  Proposed, no new value

Past four groups

Past four groups a chart takes one of two forms, with every group labeled on the chart (DR-234). Small multiples give each group a panel of its own, with the other groups faint behind it. One group in ink against gray keeps a single panel, and the title names the group in ink.

Neither form adds a color. The ghost lines reuse the light Stone fill on Newsprint and a dark Stone step on Charcoal, at 1 px, and repeat lines drawn in full in their own panels. The gray lines of the second form take the context ink the release already holds.

06  Proposed, values from Rule 10.10

The grade line code

V7's line code for the evidence grade of a connection: solid for documented and well supported, dashed for plausible, dash-dot for contested and dotted for hypothesis, with the record square and the hollow line Gate 1 added (M1). Each pattern is a multiple of the line's width, as Rule 10.10 sets it, so the 6 px lit route keeps its texture.

The tokens carry each pattern per 1 px of width, and the kit stretches it so a line starts and ends on ink. Phase 0 read V7's renderer's dash arrays as 9 and 4 for plausible, 9, 3, 2 and 3 for contested, and 2 and 4 for hypothesis. On a 2 px line the tokens draw 8 and 4, then 8, 3, 2 and 3, then round dots 5 px apart: the same textures at the guide's weights.

No coded line drops under 2 px, and a run shorter than 28 widths prints its grade in words beside it.

Red on red

Each view carries one working red (Rule 05.3). A chart's red goes to the one point its title names, third in line after an error the reader must fix and a step the reader can take now. Master Brief tension 14 asks what happens when a chart's red meets a red the view already holds.

The answer needs no new token. The chart's point yields to the subject ink and keeps its bold label and its place, as the take-part mark and the intervention marker yield in Rule 11.16. The title still names it, so the reader loses nothing.

In the grayscale capture the red bar and the Stone bars print almost the same gray (Chromium's filter gives 0.371 and 0.386), so the keyline, the bold label and the title carry the point in black-and-white print.
