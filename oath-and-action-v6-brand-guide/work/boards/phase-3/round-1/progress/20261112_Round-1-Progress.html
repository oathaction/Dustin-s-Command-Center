The four sketches

Four forms get a small sketch with invented, labeled data in place of a finished chart: two the guide tells builders to avoid, and two it keeps for reference. Each is shown with the heading the guide prints over it, photographed at a 1440 px window.

Rule 14.24 · A form to avoid

Avoid radar charts, whose shapes change with the order of their spokes.

Rule 14.44 · A reference form

Lay each year over the same months to show a season’s pattern.

Rule 14.45 · A form to avoid

Avoid streamgraphs, whose layers float on a baseline no one can read.

Rules 14.1 to 14.14, for every chart

Chapter 14 opens with 14 rules that hold for every chart. Each rule has two plates, the framed examples under it: a correct one and a failure. Both are photographed from today’s guide at a 1440 px window, side by side where they fit and one above the other where they do not. The chapter’s opening plate comes first.

The chapter’s opening plate

Rule 14.1 Start from the reader’s question, then choose the form.

Rule 14.2 Build every chart in one anatomy, from evidence label to table.

Rule 14.3 Label the data directly, and use a key only when labels cannot fit.

Rule 14.4 Start bars and columns at zero, keep one scale per axis, and size circles by area.

Rule 14.5 Give each color in a chart one job from the four data palettes.

Rule 14.6 Spend the view’s one red on the point the title names.

Rule 14.7 Tell parties apart by name, place and shape, with no red and blue.

Rule 14.8 Past four groups, split into small multiples or set one group in ink against gray.

Rule 14.9 Show the publisher’s uncertainty, and rank only where a gap passes the margin of error.

Rule 14.10 Choose the window from the question, and draw an inauguration only as a dated note.

Rule 14.11 Mark a gap, a break in a series or a preliminary value in the drawing.

Rule 14.12 Redraw for a phone only when the form must change, and keep the data and finding.

Rule 14.13 Carry every chart’s table as live text, and publish the still image first.

Rule 14.14 Export every chart from the kit, so its files match the page.
