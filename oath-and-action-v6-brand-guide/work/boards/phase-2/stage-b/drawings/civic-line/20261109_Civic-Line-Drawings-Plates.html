Pixel-fit pillar icons

Each fit is drawn pixel by pixel and keeps every feature and red placement of its master, with the red as its own shape. Under 48 px the black edition is the one in use (D2-497). At 24 px, fit A has a 2 px line and fit B a 1 px line. At 16 px there is one fit: a 2 px line there closes Connect's lens, Track's pin and lens, the book's doubled edge and Act's finger.

What 2x screens get. On a 2x screen a fit's pixels double into hard steps, while the master at 24 px draws 48 device pixels with a 1.26 device-pixel line that passes the pixel-fit check's width and gap tests. At 16 px the master's line falls to 0.84 device pixels and fails the width test, and Act's parts close to 0.7 px apart. Recommendation: on 2x screens, 24 px icons draw the master and 16 px icons keep the pixel fit; 1x screens draw the fit at both sizes. The engineer builds the switch by screen resolution, as for the identity designer's end mark.
