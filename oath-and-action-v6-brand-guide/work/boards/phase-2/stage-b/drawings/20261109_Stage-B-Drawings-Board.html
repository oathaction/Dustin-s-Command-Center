How to read a card

Naming testThe creative director looked at each option with no label, at 48 px for a Civic Line drawing and 24 px for a utility icon, and wrote down what a first-time reader would most likely call it. Since the creative director knew the labels, the reading stands in for a first-time reader’s. Real readers see some of these drawings, among them the pillar icons and the end mark, in the combined reader check after you answer (DR-143). A Civic Line verdict runs from Names the subject through Names it nearly, where the label settles it, and Recognized with the label to Misleads without the label. A utility icon’s verdict is Names it, Names it nearly or Partly, where a reader names a near subject. The maker’s verdict is the designer’s own reading.

Look-alike figureA look-alike figure says how alike two drawings look at 24 px on the engineer’s check, on a scale from 0 to 1, where 1 means the same drawing. Among the 7,381 pairs of settled Civic Line drawings, one in 20 measures 0.871 or more, and 74 pairs, one in 100, measure 0.913 or more, most of them drawings built on a shared base, such as a page or a document with a different mark on it. The board flags a figure of 0.913 or more, which asks for a look by eye, and marks one from 0.871 up as one to watch. Before CL-014 B was redrawn, it measured 0.950 against R-46 Expand the House, and the creative director set the two side by side and found a reader will not take one for the other.

Pixel fitA pixel fit is a drawing redrawn for one small size so its lines land on whole pixels and its edges stay crisp. The engineer’s pixel-fit check draws each file at 1x and at 2x. Its tests include lines at least one device pixel wide, straight edges on pixel edges, parts at least a pixel apart, no hole filling in and, where a drawing has a set line weight, a main line that matches it.

1x and 2x screensA device pixel is one dot of the screen. A 1x screen, such as many desktop monitors, gives each pixel of a drawing one device pixel. A 2x screen, the sharper kind on phones and many laptops, gives it two across and two down, so edges look smoother and a fit’s pixels show as larger steps.

Within DR-135On November 8 you approved redrawing the domes and columns in six icons while keeping every other part and its red (DR-135). An option marked Within DR-135 does that. An option marked Outside DR-135 changes more, and its card says what.

ScoresThe creative director scores each card from 1 to 5 on six criteria: craft, clarity, brand fidelity, usefulness, evidence honesty and accessibility. The score tables shorten brand fidelity to Brand and evidence honesty to Evidence. The guide’s bar for shipping is 4 or more on every criterion, so the board boxes and names any score under 4 (D2-499).
