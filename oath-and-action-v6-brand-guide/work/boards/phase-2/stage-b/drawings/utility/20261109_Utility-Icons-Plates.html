Utility icons: the family card, then the options for each drawing

Eighteen drawings for seventeen interface actions, as the accessibility audit lists them (DR-134, DR-146). The family card comes first and settles one question for the whole set: bare actions, or every action in a ring. Each card after it offers bare pictures only: three where three real choices exist, fewer with a one-line reason (D2-622).

Construction: a 24-unit grid with a 2 px line at 24 px (the token --oa-component-icon-utility-line-medium) and a 1.5 px line on its own 16 px master. Round caps and joins, one color through currentColor, no red. Every straight line sits on whole pixels. The first row of each option is true size; capture this page at device scale 1. Specimen words are examples from the audit.

Naming at 24 px is the designer's own reading of each option with no label, before any reader sees it. Likeness is the engineer's look-alike check at 24 px: it watches from 0.871 (95th percentile of the pairs among the 122 Civic Line masters) and flags from 0.913 (99th). Each card prints the figures between its own options.
