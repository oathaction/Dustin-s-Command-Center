Utility icons: three options for each drawing

Eighteen drawings for seventeen interface actions, as the accessibility audit lists them, with three options each (DR-134, DR-146). Every option of one action shares the grid and the line, so the set stays one family whichever you pick. The options differ in the picture.

Construction: a 24-unit grid with a 2 px line at 24 px (the token --oa-component-icon-utility-line-medium) and a 1.5 px line on its own 16 px master. Round caps and joins, one color through currentColor, no red. Every straight line sits on whole pixels. The first row of each option is true size; capture this page at device scale 1. Specimen words are examples from the audit.

Naming at 24 px is the designer's own reading of each option with no label, before any reader sees it. Likeness is the engineer's look-alike check at 24 px against the 122 Civic Line masters: it watches from 0.871 (95th percentile of the master pairs) and flags from 0.913 (99th).
