Decision 2 of 2

For the record: D2-976 · DR-136

Tiles under the 48 px floor

On November 8 you set 48 px as the smallest size for a topic or atlas symbol, with 24 px allowed only for the 41 that read there. A tile, the square, rounded square or circle that holds a drawing as an app icon or an avatar, draws its symbol at about two thirds of its own size. So a one-color tile from 48 px draws its symbol at 32 to 34 px, under that floor.

The tile sizes came from the October review, which read every drawing at 32 px before you set the floor. Rule 10.5 now names the small one-color tiles as the one exception:

A one-color tile may go smaller, from 48 px square and 52 px rounded or round, which holds its drawing at 32 to 34 px. This is the one place a drawing off the list of 41 goes under Rule 10.4’s 48 px floor, and it is open only to the 94 drawings that reviewers could still name at 32 px. The other 30 take one-color tiles from 72, 76 and 80 px, which hold them at 48 px or more.

Rule 10.5, third paragraph

The other answer

Public meeting, a topic symbol off the list of 41, at actual size on Newsprint. The tile is Charcoal, the default for app icons and avatars.

Recommendation

Keep the exception. Avatars and small app tiles show at 32 to 48 px, and on a tile the drawing sits on a solid ground of its own.

The other answer gives all 124 drawings one-color tiles from 72 px square, 76 px rounded and 80 px round, which keeps the 48 px floor with no exception and rules out small avatars.

What you approve by name

Keep the one-color tile exception in Rule 10.5.
