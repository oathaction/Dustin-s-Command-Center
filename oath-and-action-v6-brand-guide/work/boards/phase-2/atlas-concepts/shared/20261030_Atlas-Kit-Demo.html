R10: Safe seats shift accountability to small primary electorates

In a safe seat, the primary decides who wins, and few people vote in it. With little threat in November, officeholders answer to that smaller electorate and face less pressure from the wider public. Officials who cannot easily be removed can then write election rules that keep seats safe.

How to read a wire

The line's texture gives its evidence grade. A plus sign means the two variables move the same way; a minus sign means they move opposite ways. Neither means good or bad.

  • well supported
  • plausible
  • contested
