04 Signs, ends and delay

One step, fully dressed

Step 2 of LoopR10, read from the model. The sign rides the line in a pointing tag; one tick counts the delay; the label sits clear of every stroke.

Key

  • Pill: a variable. Its code stands alone because this key names the type.
  • Tag: the step’s sign, pointing the way the step runs.
  • Tick: one per delay band. One tick: under 2 years.
  • Texture: the grade. Solid here: well supported.

A plus sign means the two variables move the same way. It does not mean the change is good.

Sign tags

Tags 16 × 20 px on 2 px lines, 17 × 21 px on 3 px lines, 22 × 26 px on the lit route. The sign is drawn, its bars as heavy as the line up to 3 px. Never colored.

Arrowheads

One closed, filled head, sized by weight. The line stops at the head’s base. Tip 2 px off the target’s edge.

2 px line: head 10 × 8 px

3 px line: head 13 × 11 px

6 px line: head 22 × 18 px

Delay

Ticks 2 px wide, 5 px apart, 12 px after the tail, with the words in the label.

Under 2 years: 1 tick

2 to 10 years: 2 ticks

Over 10 years: 3 ticks
