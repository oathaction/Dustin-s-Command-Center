Systems Atlas · Loop catalog

18 feed themselves

Snowball loops. A change comes back around and builds on itself, turn after turn.

5 push back

Thermostat loops. A change sets off a correction that pulls the system back.

Atlas V7 names 23 feedback loops: chains of cause that come back to where they began. Each one is drawn by the same rule. A loop that feeds itself is one turn of a spiral that returns to its first variable one lap further out. A loop that pushes back is a ring that closes on its start.

Every snowball is drawn the same. The shape shows the kind of loop and never its speed or strength.

Start at twelve o'clock

Follow the line clockwise. Each disc is a variable, shown by its ID; the list under each shape gives its name.

Each segment is one step

One variable moves the next. The line's texture shows how strong the evidence is for that step.

  • Documented or well supported: on the public record, or strong studies agree on the direction
  • Plausible: a coherent mechanism with some evidence
  • Contested: credible studies disagree
  • Hypothesis: proposed, not yet supported
  • Sketched: V7 names no numbered step

A snowball comes back further out

The outlined disc repeats the first ID one lap out: the loop has returned to where it started, one turn later, and each turn builds on the last. The outward step means one more turn and never an amount.

A thermostat closes on its start

A change sets off a push back toward where it began. Under each shape, a line in words says which way the loop is running now, how long one turn takes and which step is least certain.
