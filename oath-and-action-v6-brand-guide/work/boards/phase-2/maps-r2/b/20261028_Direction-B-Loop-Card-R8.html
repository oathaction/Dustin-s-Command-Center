ReformR-30More homes allowed by right near jobs

On this loop it acts at L39, Owners' equity drives opposition to new housing and A5, Housing scarcity, marked in red. Outside this loop it also acts at LE17, Housing scarcity raises rents and prices.

Homes that meet clear, published rules could be built near jobs and transit without a separate discretionary approval, such as a hearing where neighbors can block each project. Renters and young households would gain within three to ten years as more homes are built and rents ease. Owners in high-demand places would give up some price growth and some control over their neighborhoods.

Evidence for the problem: Well supported. Evidence for the remedy: Plausible, a coherent mechanism with some evidence.

Confidence in the effects (V7): high where binding; medium for US statutes.
