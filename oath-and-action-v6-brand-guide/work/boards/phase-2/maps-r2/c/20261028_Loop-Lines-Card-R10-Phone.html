Where a reform acts

ReformR-01 All-party primaries with ranked general elections

Red marks where R-01 acts on this view: L25, L08 and C2.

In a safe seat the general election is predictable, so the party primary picks the winner (L08: Safe seats make the primary decisive). That weakens the threat incumbents face from the wider public (L25: Decisive primaries reduce general-election pressure). An all-party primary sends more than one candidate to November, which aims to lower C2: How often the primary decides the winner and raise C3: Risk of losing office. A top-two design advances two candidates; a top-four design advances four and can be paired with a ranked-choice general election. The reform sits on loop R10 (Safe seats shift accountability to small primary electorates), loop B4 (Electoral consequences can improve public performance) and loop R11 (Party loyalty weakens consequences for breaking norms).
