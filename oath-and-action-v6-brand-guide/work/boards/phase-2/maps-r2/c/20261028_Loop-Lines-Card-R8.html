Where a reform acts

ReformR-30 More homes allowed by right near jobs

Red marks where R-30 acts on this view: L39 and A5. It also acts on LE17, housing scarcity raises rents and prices, which runs from Housing scarcity to cost of essentials in the economy layer, outside this loop.

Scarcity raises the value of existing homes (L38: Scarcity raises existing home values), and owners with equity at stake organize to block new supply (L39: Owners' equity drives opposition to new housing). Allowing homes by right moves the decision out of meetings dominated by those owners. The aim is to reduce A5: Housing scarcity and, through it, rents and prices (LE17: Housing scarcity raises rents and prices). The reform works through loop R8 (Housing scarcity strengthens owners' incentive to restrict building).
