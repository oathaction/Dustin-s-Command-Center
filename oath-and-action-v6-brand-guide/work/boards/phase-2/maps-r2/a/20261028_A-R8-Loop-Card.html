Scarce homes build owners’ equity, which keeps homes scarce

LoopR8Housing scarcity strengthens owners' incentive to restrict building

Read it aloud“Housing scarcity raises homeowners' equity, which raises housing scarcity.”

Feeds itself

Reinforcing loop. V7: “A feedback loop whose returning effect amplifies the first change. Improvement or decline can reinforce itself.”

What the return line means

L39, Owners' equity drives opposition to new housing. Owners whose wealth sits in their home attend zoning hearings and object to new construction nearby, which keeps supply short.

Least certain step

No single step; every step is well supported. V7: “it is the weaker step because equity is one motive among several for opposing construction.”

One turn

Over 10 years. V7 gives a loop the delay of its slowest step.

Now

Running up. V7 tracks it by “Owner-renter net worth gap (SCF) 1989 to 2022”.

Where a reform acts

ReformR-30 More homes allowed by right near jobs

Red marks where R-30 acts in this loop: L39, Owners' equity drives opposition to new housing; A5, Housing scarcity. It also acts outside this loop on LE17, Housing scarcity raises rents and prices. Reform R-06 also acts on L39; V7 does not list it on this loop.

Evidence that the problem exists: Well supported. Evidence that the remedy works: Plausible. Confidence in the effects, in V7’s words: “high where binding; medium for US statutes”.
