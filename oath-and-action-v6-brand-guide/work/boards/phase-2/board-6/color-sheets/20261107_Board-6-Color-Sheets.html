Board 6 · Color and identity designer

Six color schemes for Oath & Action, with today’s palette beside them

You asked which colors could lead if red stepped back from the front. Each sheet keeps Newsprint, Charcoal, the three faces, the hinge and the approved marks, and changes the lead color and its partners. A seventh sheet shows today’s palette at the same size.

What we recommend

We recommend Patina. It does every job with one hex on Newsprint and on Charcoal, reads as civic, and carries the lowest party reading of the six. It costs two token changes, shown on its sheet: the focus ring moves to Charcoal, and the diverging scale’s green end moves to Brass. If you want to keep the urgency red gives the brand today, Signal Orange is the second choice, and it changes no token.
