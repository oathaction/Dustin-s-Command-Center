A standing rule on examples

Our systems atlas flags two of its 50 reforms as tied to your own work: Reform R-01, “All-party primaries with ranked general elections”, and Reform R-02, “Ranked-choice voting for single-winner races”. The guide prints no disclosure, so the studio keeps its examples off both.

The brand and evidence team asks for one rule that holds everywhere.

What round 2 found and fixed

The reviewers read the frozen page you saw as version 6. These are the faults a reader or a builder would notice, each with its fix, now in version 7.

What a reader sees

  • Red stays off Prussian

    In the dark theme a form field sat on Prussian, so an error’s red bar touched it, the red and blue pair Rule 05.3 rules out. A field on Charcoal now takes Charcoal inside its light edge, where the bar measures 4.49 to 1. The guide check now fails any red pixel beside Prussian, in both themes, after it presses Send in every form.

  • Our marks rest whole

    The players for the wordmark and the lockmark rested on their first frames, so a reader who never pressed Play met split words and empty squares. Each now rests on its master (Rule 17.3).

  • Rule 12.4, rewritten

    As you approved on November 11, the hinge arcs retire. An open area stays plain, or carries the large crop of our ampersand or a watermark.

What a builder uses

  • A production test Simulated

    One reviewer played a panel of three in turn. As its developer, they built an event sign-up card from chapters 18 and 26 and tokens.css alone, then switched it to dark mode, and the guide failed them twice. The chapters said to put a surface class on the body, and that class keeps one ground in both themes, so the card stayed light. The token block each chapter offered as a second route missed 37 properties its code reads, so the form drew in a fallback font with a send button under 44 px. Rules 26.3 and 26.6 now leave the body with no surface class, and tokens.css is the one route. The panel is simulated and untested with real people.

  • A token table you can scan

    Its 17 groups stay closed until a reader opens one, so the table stands 1,057 px tall at 1440 px, where it ran 175,377 px. From a plate width of 40rem each group is a real table (Rule 26.7).

  • Code panels break their lines

    Each declaration and each long attribute takes its own line, so the longest line fell from 3,307 characters to 193, and a panel shows about 40 lines before it scrolls.

  • Token release 6.0.0-gate2.8

    The error bar’s token takes the name oa.color.state.error.bar, and the old name works until 6.1.0. Every description a reader sees, across all 504 tokens, is in plain words.

What the evidence says

  • W3C’s words in W3C’s order

    Four criteria printed their parts out of order, so success criterion 3.3.7 read as if filling in a field for the reader were an exception. All 55 now print from a stored copy of the Recommendation fetched from w3.org, and the build stops if the page differs from it (Rule 19.1).

  • Met means a rule says so

    A criterion now reads Met only where a rule states what it asks. Met fell from 37 to 33; eight are met in part, nine are gaps and five cover things no product does yet.

  • Loop R1 states its grade

    The guide’s main example of a loop about money in politics read as a finding. Its heading now names the loop in quotation marks, with a line under it (Rule 18.22): “Our atlas grades this loop plausible: its middle step is the least certain, and it runs only on issues where large donors and middle-income voters want different things.”

  • Examples left the subject of your work

    Chapter 18’s samples now come from the housing loop, Loop R8, and Reform R-30, with their real grades and a dated source. The decision above asks whether this becomes a standing rule.

Calls the studio made that you may overrule

The studio made these five calls inside the plan you approved. Each stands unless you say otherwise.

  • Fields on Charcoal take Charcoal

    For the record: D2-1544 · D2-1469 · D2-1562

    An earlier call let an error’s red bar touch a Prussian field in the dark theme, because the pair measures 3.19 to 1, over the 3 to 1 a mark needs. The fix pass withdrew that call, since Rule 05.3 gives this reason: “Red never sits on Prussian, where it measures 3.19 to 1 and reads as a party pair of red and blue.” The field’s ground moved to Charcoal, so a field shows by its light edge (Rule 18.17). The selected band on Charcoal stays Prussian, and the guide check keeps red away from it.

  • The ask stands on its own row

    For the record: D2-1545 · D2-1592

    A primary button’s hover turns Prussian, and in Rule 18.1’s plate it sat 12 px from the red ask. The hover stays as our token plan sets it, and the ask now has a row of its own. Rule 18.1 says: “The ask stands on a row of its own, apart from any primary button, whose hover turns Prussian on Newsprint, so the ask’s red never meets Prussian (Rule 05.3).”

  • A mark’s player rests on the mark

    For the record: D2-1548 · D2-1563

    Rule 17.3 now says: “For the wordmark or the lockmark, the player rests on the master itself, so a reader who never presses Play still sees the whole mark; Play then steps to the first frame and runs to the master.” A player of a state or a mechanism still rests on its first frame, where the change starts.

  • tokens.css is the one route

    For the record: D2-1549 · D2-1639 · D2-266

    The engineer chose one route over a complete copy of each chapter’s tokens. A copy with both themes would hold most of tokens.css, and one without them would teach a product with no dark mode. Each chapter’s block stays for reading, and the words above it say: “It holds no themes and no surface classes, so it cannot stand in for tokens.css.” Rule 26.5 starts every page from tokens.css.

  • Closed groups for the token table

    For the record: D2-1557 · D2-1641

    Each of the 17 groups stays closed until a reader opens it or a search finds a match in it, and a list of the groups heads the table. Under a plate width of 40rem, as on a phone, the rows stay cards (Rule 26.7). An open table with the rail out of the way would still run roughly 35,000 px, as the craft team measured.
