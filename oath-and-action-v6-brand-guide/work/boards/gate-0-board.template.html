The atlas bases

Your four favored concepts cover the overview (09 The Circuit), the layers (03 Field Manual), the actors (10 Who Can Act) and the evidence (11 Register). The data visualization lead proposes 07 Loop Card as a fifth base, because five of the six worked examples are single loops or interventions.

What changes: V7's line code (dotted hypothesis, dashed plausible), typed ID chips in place of bare IDs, ordinal effects shown as words or pips with V7's qualifiers, costs drawn beside gains, and a static table view behind every interactive map.

Phase 1 proposal

Phase 1 produces

  1. A research ledger with every reference re-verified, civic identity benchmarks, interactive references, and the license position on a logo built from Inter Tight and Lora outlines.
  2. A strategy board: positioning, audiences, brand architecture, the Act pattern that separates process pieces from labeled position pieces, a written nonpartisan rule set, and the voice line revision beside the original.
  3. An identity board with one panel per element, each marked as a defect fix or an option. It includes two ampersand options: an optical tune with a true small master, or a drawn ampersand that keeps the silhouette and becomes Oath & Action's own.
  4. A symbol family board: one skeleton per pillar in two directions, geometric solid and line with one accent, shown filled, as line and as tile at 16 to 96 px. It draws on the icon styles you shared today.
  5. A systems marks board, the guide architecture and navigation, the token architecture, and Master Brief V2.

You decide at Gate 1

  • Strategy and the Act pattern
  • The voice line revision
  • One symbol direction and the topic grammar
  • Each identity refinement, accepted or declined
  • The red ampersand on Prussian
  • Evidence line code, grade words, ID chips and the ordinal effect mark
  • Architecture, navigation and tokens
  • The atlas bases, including 07 Loop Card
