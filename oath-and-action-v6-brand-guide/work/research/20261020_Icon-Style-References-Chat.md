Dustin shared five collages of icon styles he likes on October 20, 2026, during Phase 0. They arrived as images in the conversation and are not saved as files in this project. If Dustin adds the files, store them in `source/icon-style-references/` and give each a row in the rights ledger.

## What this means for Phase 1

These references agree with the Phase 0 audits on three points.

1. **One skeleton, several treatments.** Collage 5's Line, Duo, Solid and Flat panel shows the structure the creative director proposed: draw each pillar and topic once, then derive sanctioned treatments from the same drawing. Phase 1 builds the symbol family this way.
2. **Filled geometric silhouettes lead.** Collages 1, 2 and 3 favor bold filled forms built from circles, squares and quarter circles. V5's topic set is already a 48-unit filled family, and study 06 supplies the geometry. This supports a filled master with a line companion from 24 px up.
3. **One accent, offset or embedded.** Collage 5's offset accent and the accent node in study 08 give the system its single point of color. In Oath & Action that accent is Charcoal or Verdigris by default, with Pillar Red reserved for Act contexts.

Gradients, gloss, 3D shading and off-palette purples conflict with the flat Newsprint and Charcoal foundation. The studio takes their silhouettes and construction only.
