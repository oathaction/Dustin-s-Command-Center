## Rule that governs every reference

Master Brief section 2: reference images are for principles and mechanisms only. Study them; never trace, copy, embed or ship them. Final marks are authored vectors. A watermarked stock file is a comp preview, and the agency terms checked here allow comps only for internal layout tests.
