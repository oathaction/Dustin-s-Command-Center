# Creative Director Audit: Brand Guide V5 Polished

Phase 0, October 20, 2026, oa-creative-director. Tiles are in `work/boards/screens/v5-polished/1440-tiles/` unless named.

## Verdict

V5 Polished has a recognizable editorial identity in Newsprint, Charcoal and the red ampersand, and its atlas chapter models honest evidence. No chapter reaches 4 on every criterion. The largest gaps are nonpartisan reading, unlabeled illustrative material, a Signal Grid that stops signalling at working sizes, symbols without one family, and no downloads.

## 1. What to protect

- **Cover and closing.** The tagline beside a cropped red ampersand passes the cover-the-wordmark test (00-top-00.jpg, 17-closing-00.jpg).
- **The hinge** as headline, diagram and divider (02-voice-01.jpg, 04-ampersand-01.jpg). The divider before "What you can do" gives every article the same turn from explanation to action (04-ampersand-02.jpg).
- **Newsprint first, red as punctuation.** The pillar social tiles share one structure across four surfaces (13-in-use-02.jpg).
- **The measured contrast table** (05-color-01.jpg) and **type craft shown before and after** (06-type-01.jpg).
- **The voice method**, "We publish the mechanism / Not the mood," with its banned words (02-voice-01.jpg). Its examples need replacing (section 2).
- **Signal Grid geometry, the nine-stage tracker and the composer** (07-grid-00.jpg, 07-grid-02.jpg, 07-grid-03.jpg).
- **The seal's restraint**: "No crest, no shield, no founding date, no ring of words" (04-ampersand-02.jpg).
- **The atlas evidence grammar**: labeled dashed hypotheses and qualification panels (16-atlas-system-01.jpg).
- **The photography shot list** (10-photography-00.jpg) and **the report reader**, which returns focus to Close (reports-1440/06.jpg).

## 2. Where V5 falls short

**Across the guide**

- **Nonpartisan test.** Red sits on Prussian in the Voice hinge headlines, beside "Never red against blue" (02-voice-01.jpg), and again in the C3 avatar (04-ampersand-01.jpg), an elections chart with a Prussian "General election" line and a red point (11-data-00.jpg), the home page (13-in-use-01.jpg) and the atlas reform box (16-atlas-system-02.jpg). Every In Use example serves one open-primaries campaign, and the script ends "I ask the committee to report H.4212 favorably" (13-in-use-01.jpg). The only atlas reform shown is "Taxing inherited gains and capital income," framed in red. The video cover asks "Who wrote the rule you are angry about?" (13-in-use-02.jpg).
- **Evidence honesty.** One global note covers every invented item. Testimonials name people, towns and jobs (10-photography-02.jpg). A letter signed by Dustin Cathcart addresses "Chair Whitcomb" (13-in-use-04.jpg). Illustrative data carries real-looking sources: "legislature.ma.gov bill history, pulled October 17" (13-in-use-01.jpg) and "Town budget, fiscal 2026, table 4" (14-reports-01.jpg). Voice models the specific rule with an unsourced statistic about 23 states (02-voice-01.jpg).
- **Usefulness.** No download or copy control exists. The OA_* names appear only in a table (15-standards-01.jpg).

**By chapter**

- **03 Identity.** Outlined tagline and URL symbols (tl-1, tl-2a, tl-2b, url-1) lose descenders at the viewBox edge (sprite-sheet.png), visible in L5 and L6 (03-identity-01.jpg). L2 hangs a small ampersand off ACTION.
- **07 Grid.** Rules A and B draw identical grids (07-grid-01.jpg), and six encodings have no precedence. At kicker and tracker-row size the lit node disappears (07-grid-02.jpg, 09-topic-icons-02.jpg). Active recolors the lever, which misuse forbids (07-grid-03.jpg). Loading loops, which Motion forbids (12-motion-01.jpg).
- **08 Pillar Marks.** Book, staircase, chain and arrow use four constructions (08-pillar-marks-00.jpg). Connect reads as a hyperlink and Act as a growth arrow, and Act collapses at 16 px.
- **09 Topic Icons.** Solid and outline drawings mix. The dome and temple are clichés, Open primary reads as sign-in, and Taxes and budget is a bar chart (09-topic-icons-00.jpg). Report page 6 uses them as role icons (reports-1440/06.jpg), and the social tile specimen overflows (09-topic-icons-02.jpg).
- **10 Photography.** Grey boxes stand where photographs belong (10-photography-00.jpg).
- **11 Data.** "Closed primaries are the largest group." sits over bars where Open is 18 and Closed 15, confirmed in the SVG (11-data-00.jpg), and the one-pager repeats it (13-in-use-03.jpg). The chapter has no chart chooser, the title is each chart's only accessible text, and labels fall to about 6 px at 390 px (390-composites/11-data-00.jpg).
- **13 In Use.** Shrunken desktop pages put 173 text items under 10 px. The L8 avatar turns O and A red because `.cm.c-newsprint` sets color to Pillar Red (13-in-use-02.jpg). The banner grid is cut off (13-in-use-04.jpg), and the one-pager spends red four times (13-in-use-03.jpg).
- **14 Reports.** "100 units" in an unnamed town reads as a template (14-reports-00.jpg).
- **15 Standards.** The checklist skips R4 and R10, and R1 collides with atlas loop R1 (15-standards-02.jpg, 16-atlas-system-01.jpg).
- **16 Atlas.** Embedded SVG images with their own folio and heading weight fail the distinctiveness test and crop at 390 px (390-composites/16-atlas-system-00.jpg). Atlas V7 uses a condensed face and a darker red (atlas-v7/00.jpg).
- **Ambition.** No page starts from a reader's question. V7 opens with "Why did my rent go up?" (atlas-v7/00.jpg).

## 3. Opportunities for V6

1. **Decodable Signal Grid.** Propose at Gate 1: position names the pillar first, fill codes tracker progress only, color stops coding content type, and each encoding gets a minimum size (40 px for progress). A one-line key sits beside every signalled grid.
2. **One symbol family from one skeleton.** Draw each pillar skeleton on the 123.5-unit field and derive filled and 2 px line treatments from it. Redraw the topic icons in that line grammar with a Charcoal accent node, red only in Act contexts.
3. **A nonpartisan rule set.** Outside locked lockups (question 2), red never shares a figure with Prussian. Examples rotate across issues. Pending question 1, action asks name a process step and leave the position to the reader.
4. **Labels at the point of use.** An "Illustrative" chip beside each invented person, quote, figure and source, and a claim line under each chart title: finding, association, estimate or illustration.
5. **The hinge as data grammar.** "The rule & the result" becomes the signature chart: a dated rule-change marker, the outcome before and after, the source beneath. The chart chooser builds from it.
6. **The atlas inside the identity.** Live SVG in guide tokens, typed ID chips, a stacked 390 px layout, and V7's question-first entry as the newcomer model.
7. **The package on the page.** Downloads, copy controls, dated filenames and a "For builders" note for each mark.
8. **Work at reading size.** Documentary frames with rights labels, 1:1 crops of desktop specimens, and the 13 px floor everywhere.

**Principles to take**

- Mark the date a rule changed on the chart (data-viz-03 #38).
- Sorted dumbbells with direct labels and a two-entry key (data-viz-03 #37).
- Axis direction in words, "Better" and "Worse" (data-viz-04 #57).
- An ordinal scale written as words, weak to strong (data-viz-04 #62).
- Chart choice by relationship (data-viz-01 #5).
- Proximity, similarity and enclosure as decoding rules (data-viz-05 #67).
- A loop legend beside the diagram with signs, R and B badges and delay marks (design-i-like-02 #25).
- Exploded map layers (design-i-like-02 #23, design-i-like-01 #13).
- A fictional-data label inside the chart (design-i-like-01 #11).
- A question-first front page, without its condensed face or dome (design-i-like-02 #19).

**To avoid**

- Stock dashboards, neon and 3D charts (data-viz-01 #1 to #3, #14, #15; data-viz-02 #31; data-viz-04 #54, #58, #60; data-viz-05 #65, #66, #72).
- Red-blue diverging scales and red against green (data-viz-04 #50, #55).
- Hairball networks (design-i-like-02 #26; data-viz-04 #59).
- Crypto cubes and stock icon sets (design-i-like-03 #39, #42 to #48; design-i-like-04 #49).
- Red and navy patterns (design-i-like-01 #10).
