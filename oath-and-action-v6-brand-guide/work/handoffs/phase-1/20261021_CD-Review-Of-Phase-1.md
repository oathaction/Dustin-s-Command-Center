**The red rule.** The wordmark's ampersand and the lockmark's lever are signature red and do not count. Each view (one screen, card, slide or printed page) then carries one working red, in this order of precedence: an error the reader must fix; the step a reader can take now; the one point the headline names, which in a systems figure is where a reform acts. A hinge headline's ampersand uses the view's working red. The hinge divider's ampersand is Charcoal. The Act handle is red only when the Act mark is itself the call to act, as on a social tile. Red never sits on Prussian.

**Consistent.** Direction A's square corners match Inter Tight 800's flat terminals, and its node repeats the lockmark and hinge nodes, with red on the node that acts, as on the lever; Phase 2 sets one node-to-stem ratio. "From spectators to stewards." appears only in the strategy decision, beside V4.2's retirement record.

## 3. Distinctiveness and ambition

With the wordmark covered, the systems pages still read as Oath & Action through the typed chips and V7's line code, and so does the symbol board's story list, with one node in the same place in every mark. A serif red ampersand between heavy sans words is the system's strongest asset, and the strategy board's hinge headlines hide it.

Still generic:

- **The guide shell** could be any documentation site. Phase 2 should mark the parts with the pillar marks and set the hinge divider between each rule and its correct application.
- **The condensed 7.5 to 1 wordmark** is a squeezed Inter Tight, about 25 percent wider than the references it answers (5.7 to 6.3 to 1) and close to campaign-sign lettering. Its gain is height, 1.26 times at the same width.
- **Ampersand B** keeps Lora's silhouette, and its 1.5 to 1 contrast pulls it toward the sans. In Phase 2, restore contrast toward 1.7 to 1 and set the crossing on the hinge diagram's center line, so pivot and nodes share one line.
- **Direction A's Act mark** at 16 px reads close to "01" or a D (0.55). Test it with readers beside chapter numbers.

One addition would raise the gate: the symbol board's 1080 Act post rebuilt with the recommended set (ampersand B, both widths, the solid hinge, Direction A, the red rule), wordmark covered, so Dustin judges one identity before 37 questions. It blocks nothing.
