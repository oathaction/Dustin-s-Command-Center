# Creative director notes for identity

Phase 1, October 21, 2026, oa-creative-director, for the design director. V5 masters stay in force until Dustin approves a change by name.

## Decision or verdict

- **(a) Wordmark.** Lead with a condensed wordmark. Keep the current proportions on the board as the control.
- **(b) Ampersand.** Lead with a drawn, proprietary ampersand. Keep an optical tune of the Lora glyph as fallback.

A condensed wordmark would need a redrawn ampersand to fit it.

## Scores

I score the drawings when they reach the board.

## Reasons

**Condensed.** The wordmarks in p01, p07, p08, p13 and the Prussian banner mockup are 5.7 to 6.3 times as wide as their ink is tall (`work/build/scripts/phase-1/cd_wordmark_proportions.py`). The V5 master is 9.7 cap heights wide. p07 recurs five times in the PDF. The square poster is the exception, a wide sans stacked on two lines. In the same width, condensed letters gain about 1.5 times the cap height, which helps phone mastheads. Before launch, a change of width costs the least it ever will.

**Drawn ampersand.** It carries the brand idea (DC-05) and becomes the avatar, favicon and seal. Today it is the stock Lora Bold glyph, which anyone can set. Phase 0 measured its thin strokes at 0.41 of the cap stems, so it reads lighter than the words, and its crossing fills in at 16 px. The glyph is 0.89 as wide as it is tall, which sits wide among condensed capitals.

## Required changes: what each option must achieve

**Condensed wordmark**
1. Draw it from Inter Tight's own terminals, apex and apertures, so it sits beside Inter Tight 800 headlines as one family. Headlines stay Inter Tight, sentence case.
2. Test widths near 6 to 1 and 7.5 to 1. Choose by the 16 px test.
3. Keep O, A and C open at 16 px. Draw display, 24 and 16 px masters, each tracked for its size.
4. Make both hinge gaps equal. V5's right gap measures 0.083x.

**Control:** current letters with optical tracking and equal gaps, for a fair comparison.

**Drawn ampersand**
1. Keep the known silhouette: upper loop, crossing, bowl, rising tail. Overlay it on the original.
2. Match its weight to the letters, with a small master whose crossing stays open.
3. Fit the chosen wordmark's cap height and width.
4. Check similarity against common serif ampersands before Gate 1.

**Optical tune:** fix the clipped viewBox, even the gaps, thicken thin strokes and add a true 16 px master.

## Risks for Dustin to weigh

- Heavy condensed capitals are common on campaign signs. The serif ampersand and Newsprint keep the mark editorial, and condensed capitals stay in the wordmark only (design judgment).
- Neither drawing fixes red contrast. Pillar Red measures 2.96:1 on Newsprint, so the edge rule stays.
- Both elements are locked. Each change needs Dustin's named approval, shown side by side with the original at 16, 24, 48 and 96 px and display size.
