# Chapter 14 coverage plan: every way Oath & Action shows data

Oath & Action Brand Guide, Edition 06. Phase 2, November 6, 2026, revised the same day after the creative director's review (section 6). Written by the main session for Dustin's review on Board 4 and for the Phase 3 build. Paths are relative to `oath-and-action-v6-brand-guide/`.

## 1. What this plan settles

Dustin picked the Explainer system (DR-52) on Newsprint (DR-53) with diverging pair B (DR-54), and asked for a chapter that covers the full range of charts, maps, tables, diagrams and infographics his references show (DR-56). This plan lists every family and form, says what Oath & Action uses each one for, names real data for its specimen, and sets a tier. Chapter 14 is built in full at the start of Phase 3, before the Phase 4 applications need it (DR-57).

Rules that hold across every form:
- **Explainer's anatomy** on every chart: evidence label, sentence title with a verb matched to the grade, measure line, the figure, notes in the drawing, the source line in section 15 order, and a table or text equivalent.
- **Newsprint** is the ground, with Deep Stone for small text and a 1 px Charcoal keyline on any red mark. Charcoal is the dark mode.
- **One working red** per view (Rule 05.3). Red never encodes a series, a category or a scale.
- **Party marks** take neutral inks with the party's name printed, and arrows, position and words carry direction (DR-48, DR-58).
- **Diverging scales** use pair B, Prussian through Newsprint to Verdigris, with a boxed arrow or a printed sign carrying direction (DR-54).
- **Still image first** for Substack, email and social; interactive layers (filters, tabs, layer switchers, Chart and Table switches) on the atlas site and in reports (DR-59).
- **Data** from Atlas V7 and primary public sources; FRED copies of BLS series are labeled as FRED copies (DR-60).
- **The editor's disclosure** (D2-106), word for word, on every specimen drawn on election-rule, nomination or ranked-ballot data: the ranked-choice decision tree and maps, the registration-deadline scatter, the primaries comparison cards and the ranked ballot illustration among them.
- **No period split at January 2025** or at any inauguration date, and a time window's start printed with its reason (stretch fix pass).

## 6. Changes after the creative director's review (D2-127)

The creative director read this plan beside the stretch test (`work/handoffs/phase-2/20261106_CD-Review-Of-Stretch-Test.md`, "Coverage plan corrections") and the main session applied all 25 corrections. The plan moved from 122 to 127 entries.
- Added: an avoid entry for party-colored maps and red and blue scales; the evidence table, tracker list, plain table and one sentence in 3.15, which now covers plain words too; the implementation timeline and indexed line in 3.3; the waterfall in 3.4; the benefit-cliff line in 3.3; the roll-call grid in 3.11.
- Rules: the editor's disclosure on every election-rule specimen, and no period split at January 2025.
- Specimen data corrected: Venn and Euler (two state lists, since the parties' voters cannot overlap), Sankey (one Treasury node), bullet graph (a numeric target in law, or illustrative), bubble timeline (moved to 3.3, sized by a field the data holds), step line (statutes cited), parliament chart (the current House), dot plot and beeswarm (counted margins, with survey rates kept for the range bar), box plot (House margins by region).
- Tiers and families: the alluvial moves to reference, since the Current Population Survey cannot follow voters between elections; the gauge moves to avoid; the SWOT grid is dropped; the correlation heat table is dropped in favor of the adjacency matrix; duplicates merged, so each form sits in one family; the rose chart's only alternative is a bar; the sparkline table is marked as drawn in S13's totals rows.
