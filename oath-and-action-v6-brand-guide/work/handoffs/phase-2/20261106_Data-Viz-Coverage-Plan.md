# Chapter 14 coverage plan: every way Oath & Action shows data

Oath & Action Brand Guide, Edition 06. Phase 2, November 6, 2026. Written by the main session for Dustin's review on Board 4 and for the Phase 3 build. Paths are relative to `oath-and-action-v6-brand-guide/`.

## 1. What this plan settles

Dustin picked the Explainer system (DR-52) on Newsprint (DR-53) with diverging pair B (DR-54), and asked for a chapter that covers the full range of charts, maps, tables, diagrams and infographics his references show (DR-56). This plan lists every family and form, says what Oath & Action uses each one for, names real data for its specimen, and sets a tier. Chapter 14 is built in full at the start of Phase 3, before the Phase 4 applications need it (DR-57).

Rules that hold across every form:
- **Explainer's anatomy** on every chart: evidence label, sentence title with a verb matched to the grade, measure line, the figure, notes in the drawing, the source line in section 15 order, and a table or text equivalent.
- **Newsprint** is the ground, with Deep Stone for small text and a 1 px Charcoal keyline on any red mark. Charcoal is the dark mode.
- **One working red** per view (Rule 05.3). Red never encodes a series, a category or a scale.
- **Party marks** take neutral inks with the party's name printed, and arrows, position and words carry direction (DR-48, DR-58).
- **Diverging scales** use pair B, Prussian through Newsprint to Verdigris, with a boxed arrow or a printed sign carrying direction (DR-54).
- **Still image first** for Substack, email and social; interactive layers (filters, tabs, layer switchers, Chart and Table switches) on the atlas site and in reports (DR-59).
- **Data** from Atlas V7 and primary public sources; FRED copies of BLS series are labeled as FRED copies (DR-60).
