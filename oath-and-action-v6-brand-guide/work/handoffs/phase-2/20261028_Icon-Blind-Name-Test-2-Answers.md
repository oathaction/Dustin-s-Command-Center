Notes: M is the easiest sheet to match, because its door and stairs are literal and its lid is clearly lifted. On K and T, "Act" is the weakest match: the house icon (K09, T07) also has a red door, so a viewer could pick either.

## Part 3

**Most finished and professional: Sheet M.** Its single outline stroke weight, rounded corners and offset red spot are applied the same way on all ten icons, so it looks like a family drawn by one hand (close to a standard UI line set), whereas K mixes chunky strokes with solids and T mixes solid glyphs with thin-line ones like T06 and T09.

**Best for a civic news website: Sheet M.** Its light outlines sit calmly next to headlines and body text, and the off-register red blob feels editorial, like print, without shouting. Its main weakness is 16 px, where several icons (M01, M02, M05, M08) fall apart, so it should be used at 24 px or larger. T holds up better at tiny sizes but feels heavier, more like app or signage icons.
