# Icon blind name test, stage 2

Main session, October 28, 2026. Test 1 ("Name it") rerun on the finished stage 2 masters, with a new shuffle and a new rater.

## Method

- The 30 finished icons (10 per concept) rendered with no labels, file names or concept names, on Newsprint, each at its hinted 16 px master, its hinted 24 px master and its display master at 96 px. Shuffled into sheets K, M and T in `work/boards/phase-2/icons-r2/blind-test-2/`.
- A fresh agent with no project context saw only the three images. It named each icon (Part 1), matched the four pillar stories (Part 2), then judged the families (Part 3). Its full answers: `20261028_Icon-Blind-Name-Test-2-Answers.md`.
- Key: K is Public sign, M is Overprint, T is Moving part.
- One agent rater. It stands in for the reader check (Q-13) and does not replace it.

At 16 px the rater found Overprint's Explain, Testify, Act and Feedback loop fall apart, and Moving part holds best.

## The stop rule

The creative director's rule: a concept stops after stage 2 if more than two icons still fail the name test.

- **Public sign stops.** Testify, Campaign money and Feedback loop fail, and Act matches only at low confidence.
- **Moving part continues, weakly.** Feedback loop fails; Track and Act match at low confidence; Explain and Campaign money read as cameras.
- **Overprint continues.** No outright fail at 24 px; Feedback loop and Ballot are weak. It fails at 16 px.

## The rater's judgment of the families

The rater picked Overprint (M) as both the most finished family and the one it would most want on a civic news website: "the off-register red blob feels editorial, like print, without shouting." It added that Overprint should be used at 24 px or larger, and that Moving part "holds up better at tiny sizes but feels heavier, more like app or signage icons."
