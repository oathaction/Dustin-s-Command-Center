### What the result does to the map

By the unofficial count, about 62 percent of Missouri voters voted no. Under Article III, Section 52(b), H.B. 1 takes effect only "when approved by a majority of the votes cast thereon, and not otherwise", and the Supreme Court of Missouri had already held that it "did not go into effect". So the 2025 map never takes effect, and the districts the General Assembly passed in 2022 stay in force. That holds once the board of state canvassers confirms a "no" majority, which the size of the margin (about 488,000 votes) makes close to certain, though I have read no certified figure.

Two points from news reports only, not checked on any official page: the 2025 map was used for the August 4, 2026 primary (KCUR, St. Louis Public Radio), and voters also approved Amendment 6, which the county page describes as "Prohibiting the legislature from weakening citizen petition and referendum powers" (unofficial statewide, Yes 1,251,555, No 835,585, from the same St. Charles County page). Neither belongs on the plate.

### Does the plate hold?

No, on one sentence. The source line says the court "suspended the state's map for 2026 ahead of a referendum on it held November 3, 2026, after the snapshot." That was true and is now incomplete: voters turned the map down, so it never takes effect. The count itself, maps passed, still holds. Missouri's Republican-led General Assembly passed the map: House 90 to 65 on September 9, 2025, Senate 21 to 11 on September 12, and "Approved by Governor (G)" with the action dated September 29, 2025 (Missouri House, HB 1 bill actions, 2025 2nd Extraordinary Session, https://house.mo.gov/BillActions.aspx?bill=HB1&year=2025&code=S2; Revisor's history line "A.L. 2025 2nd Ex. Sess. H.B. 1"). The atlas dates the signing September 28 (RL40); the House record dates the action September 29. The plate prints neither date. The plate prints California's voter step in the body ("which voters approved"), so Missouri's voter step belongs in the body as well, in the same words. Exact change in section 5.
