The social card’s sign-off at 96 px

Proposed for board C, for approval by name (gate rule 4). Today a social card signs off with the lockmark at 48 px from its 48 px master (DR-131). A feed that shows the card 360 px wide draws that lockmark at 14.4 px, under its own 16 px floor, and its lever 2.7 px across. Rule 08.2’s test still passes there, with 5 red pixels against a floor of 4. The proposal draws the same 48 px master at exactly twice its size, 96 px, on social cards only, so every node and the lever still land on whole pixels. Covers, posters and the masthead keep 48 px. No new drawing is made.

On the card the 96 px lockmark stands 2.36 times the wordmark’s 40.7 px capitals, between the masthead’s 2.18 and the October L3’s 2.4, so the two corners keep L3’s own proportion.
