## The main session's calls

1. **DT-M1 is fixed once, in the build.** The "Files" list under a rule prints only files that ship: assets under `work/build/assets/` and the token files, each by its file name alone. Every studio source (handoffs, boards, extracts, scripts, inventories) drops out, a list left empty is removed, and the token files point to Rule 26.8's downloads, with D2-252's note kept only where no download exists yet. `guide_build.py --strict` warns on any `files` entry outside those. Makers do not edit their `files` data for this.
2. **CD-S1 and CD-S2 land in this pass**, all chapters together, so the book has two states, not three (the creative director's risk note).
3. **Openers (CD-S1).** The shell opens the `"opener"` slot (D2-965, D2-1134) to every chapter, the engineer's first job. Each opener reuses a drawing the chapter already has, at true size, with no new drawing: the creative director's list (01 the four pillar pieces, 02 the brand lines, 03 the eight lockups, 04 B at display size beside one hinge headline, 05 the seven colors with their shares, 06 the three faces, 07 the five grids, 08 the lockmark in its four places, 11 the six steps, 12 the device index). Prefer a light version or a reference to symbols the page already carries (`<use>` of the sprite) over a copy of a heavy plate. Each opener stays under 16 KB; the engineer reports the page's weight before and after. An opener is a picture (`role="img"` with alt text saying what a reader learns) unless it is live text the reader can select, such as the brand lines or the three faces.
4. **The failure bar and caption lead (CD-S2).** Every failure caption leads with "Fails Rule NN.N." in bold. The bar, "Failure case: do not publish", goes on every failure plate that draws a piece a reader could lift (a mark, a card, a page, a chart, a control), its words in `--oa-color-text-primary-on-charcoal`. A plate that draws a method rather than a piece may go without the bar; list each such plate in your notes with the reason.
5. **Rule titles (CD-S3).** One clause, about twelve words at most; the second clause moves into the rule's first sentence. Rule numbers never change. Each owner shortens every title past fifteen words in its chapters, chapters 17, 18 and 19 first, and updates any words that quote a changed title.
6. **One font weight set (CD-M1).** Rule 06.1's set is the one set: Inter 400, 600 and 700; Inter Tight 800; Lora Italic 400 and 500. Rules 06.1, 06.10 and 26.5, the code in Rule 26.5's two plates and the shell's declarations all give it. Words for a product: "A product loads the three faces with this link (Rule 06.10):". In the preview, chapter 26 and the colophon say the guide carries the font files itself. The embedded files stay unmodified (D2-1697).
7. **The front pages (CD-A1 to A5).** As the creative director wrote them: the target line is the link with the whole row as its hit area and the step's sentence in plain ink; the route heads on the part opener's Charcoal panel; the pillar row at 24 px heading the newcomer route (Rule 09.4); the colophon closing with Rule 08.2's footer sign-off, the lockmark at 24 px beside "A country that answers to its people." in Newsprint on Charcoal, the principal tagline exactly as Master Brief section 3 locks it. The engineer builds it; the identity designer checks the pillar row and the sign-off against Rules 08.2 and 09.4 once it lands, through the main session.
8. **Contrast ratios (DT-S11)** print one way everywhere: two decimals and "to 1" ("3.10 to 1"), in prose, plates and the token table.
9. **The error bar's token (AX-1).** DR-199's drawing stands. The two error tokens drop `keyline` and `keylineContrast`; their description says the bar repeats the mark and the word "Error:", which carry the error, so it takes no keyline; Rule 05.5 names the error bar as that case.
10. **Reset (DT-S8).** Rule 11.14 stands: each player's Reset names its object, such as "Reset the bill" or "Reset the lockmark", in its visible words or its accessible name.
11. **The token release.** Description changes (DT-S5, DT-S6, AX-1, AX-2) go out as one pre-release under Rule 26.8; the engineer names it and writes one change note. No token is renamed and no value changes.
12. **Chapter 19's results** come last, rerun by the accessibility lead from the final guide check on the fixed preview, so the plate describes the build Dustin reads (the creative director's risk note).
