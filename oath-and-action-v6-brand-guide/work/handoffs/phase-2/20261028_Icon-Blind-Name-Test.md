# Icon blind name test, stage 1

Main session, October 28, 2026. This is test 1 from the creative director's round-two brief ("Name it"), run on the stage 1 display masters before any hinting.

## Method

- The main session rendered the 30 display masters (10 per concept) with no labels, file names or concept names, on Newsprint, at 24 px and 96 px. It shuffled them into three sheets, P, Q and R, one per concept in a random order, with codes in a random order inside each sheet. The sheets are in `work/boards/phase-2/icons-r2/blind-test/`.
- A fresh agent with no project context looked only at the three images. It named every icon first (Part 1), then read the one-line stories of the four pillar marks and matched them to codes (Part 2). Its full answers were kept in the session scratchpad.
- Key: sheet P is Moving part, Q is Overprint, R is Public sign.
- One rater, and an agent rather than a person. This stands in for the reader check (Q-13) and does not replace it.

## Results

A subject passes when the rater named it in Part 1 as its first or second guess, and it fails when the rater named it as something else or as a system glyph the brief bans (reload, sync, notification, crop and the like).

|---|---|---|---|

## What it says

- Campaign money fails in all three concepts. A hand with a coin does not name itself at these sizes in any drawing.
- Feedback loop reads first as reload or sync in all three, which the brief bans.
- Explain fails the brief's rule in all three: a notification badge in P and Q, crop in R. The rater also said Explain and Connect could swap.
- The four pillar marks all match after one reading of their stories, most confidently in Public sign. The rater said sheet R was the easiest to read overall.
- Housing and Bill pass everywhere. Ballot passes only in Moving part. Testify passes in Moving part and Overprint.
