## 2. The stop rule

The rule from stage 1: a concept stops after stage 2 if more than two of its ten icons still fail the name test, or if a pillar mark fails the cover-the-red test or the 2x craft test.

- **Public sign stops.** Three subjects fail. Its board stays on file as a record.
- **Moving part continues as the alternative.** One subject fails and four read weakly.
- **Overprint continues.** No subject fails at 24 px. Its 16 px failures are answered by fix 1.

### Moving part

1. **Connect's bar at 4.5 units, where the notes asked for about 3.** Approved. It keeps 0.75-unit walls around the knock-out.

## 4. Recommendation to Dustin, and how the family handles 16 px

Choose Overprint. The family has two cuts.

- **From 24 px up, the Overprint line.** A 2 px line in the 24 px master and a 1.5-unit line in the display master from 48 px, round caps and joins, one Pillar Red plane filling one drawn shape and printed 1.5 units left and down. Alone, the plane is signature red; in a row only the current icon shows it (Rule 05.3).
- **At 16 px, a solid cut, in one color.** The line cut breaks down at 16 px: four icons fall apart for the rater, three pairs pass 0.60, and the masters are pixel staircases. Draw the 16 px cut in Direction A's filled grammar on the 16-unit grid as hinted vectors, with straight edges on whole pixels, curves kept as curves and a 1 px cut between parts. Start from Moving part's 16 px masters for the four pillar marks, Ballot, Bill and Housing. That set held best at 16 px in the name test and has no pair at 0.60. Redraw Testify, Campaign money and Feedback loop as filled versions of Overprint's new drawings.
- **No red at 16 px.** Sixteen-pixel icons live in rows, legends and tracker tables, where Rule 05.3 already makes them one color. A red piece at 16 px is a few pixels at 2.96:1 on Newsprint, and its keyline crowds the 1 px cut, as Moving part found. The current item at 16 px shows by its label, bold with the rule under it.

This keeps the structure Dustin approved at Gate 1 with the roles reversed: Y4 set the line from 24 px up and the solid below it, and here the line leads. Heroicons ships a solid 16 px set beside a 24 px outline set, so a split by size has a working precedent. The research lead confirms that before the guide cites it.

## 5. Ranked fixes before Dustin sees it

Each fix is one pass by the Overprint designer, in this order.

1. **The 16 px solid cut.** Draw all ten as in section 4. Pass when D1-16 finds no pair at 0.60, pixel fit reaches 0.60, no look-alike against the banned glyphs reaches 0.60, and Act scores under 0.60 against "01" with its lintel (Moving part's 16 px Act scores 0.07).
2. **Feedback loop as the loop ring.** At 24 px and in the display master, draw a closed ring with three nodes evenly spaced and one arrowhead on the ring between each pair, turning clockwise as the hinge diagram's arcs do. The plane fills the node where a reform acts. A closed ring with three stations breaks the two-arrow pattern that reload, sync, undo and redo share. Bring the ink into the band; today it sits 23 percent under the median. Check it against cycle, recycle, share and group.
3. **Ballot.** The rater saw "a red mark poking up out of a box". Raise the paper so about 7 units show above the slot, set the oval in the paper's upper half at least 2 units from its edges, and keep the plane's spill on the paper. Recheck against the printer (0.63 idle at 24 px).
4. **A similarity pass at 24 px.** Connect and Campaign money score 0.68. Widen the note to the full landscape keyline, tighten its corners to 1 px and move its disc 2 units left of center, so the pair loses its shared mirror symmetry. Then clear Track and Testify (0.61) and the idle Ballot and Bill (0.60).
5. **Rerun the name test** with a fresh rater on the finished 16 px, 24 px and display masters. The family is ready for Dustin when no subject fails, no more than one is weak at 24 px, and the 16 px cut holds.

## 6. Decisions Dustin must make

1. **The concept.** Overprint, recommended, with Moving part as the alternative and Public sign stopped.
2. **Pillar marks' treatment (Y1).** Line from 24 px up and filled at 16 px. The four skeletons stay as approved. A named approval.
3. **Y4, the line companion from 24 px.** It stands, with line and solid trading roles. Approve with decision 2.
4. **Act's lintel.** The line cut needs none. The 16 px solid cut keeps the full lintel (round one's IC-04: 0.60 against "01" without it, 0.42 with it). A named approval for the 16 px cut only.
5. **Y5's narrowing.** Topic symbols carry the node only where it is a real part of the subject; in Overprint that means the pillar marks and the loop. Recommend approval.
6. **Pillar marks with their word under 48 px.** Recommend approval. The rater matched all four only after reading their stories, and Explain still has a second reading as copy.
7. **No red at 16 px.** Sixteen-pixel icons are always one color. This narrows how Rule 05.3's icon sentences apply and leaves its words unchanged. Recommend approval.
8. **Carried from round one's panel,** valid for any concept: a standalone icon's red bare on Newsprint as signature red at 2.96:1; whether the current icon's red in a menu counts as signature red, which adds a clause to Rule 05.3 and needs his name; the superellipse for the rounded square; Charcoal as the default tile for the app icon and avatars.

Explain's skeleton needs no decision. Overprint's lidded drawing matched at medium-high confidence.

## Risks for Dustin to weigh

- Offset-accent icon sets are common in stock libraries, his own reference among them. Overprint's distinctiveness rests on the fixed offset and on the plane filling a drawn shape, and both have to hold across sixty icons.
- Signature reds stack. The Overprint social card shows the wordmark's ampersand, the icon's plane and the working red of the button in one view. Rule 05.3 allows it, and it is more red than Phase 1 showed.
- Every name test so far had one agent rater and no people (Q-13).
