# Stage A red team: craft and typography

Oath & Action Brand Guide, Edition 06. Phase 2, November 6, 2026. The craft lens of `work/handoffs/phase-2/20261106_Stage-A-Red-Team-Brief.md`, written by the craft reviewer (`oa-design-director`, as reviewer, not maker), one agent working from renders of `work/boards/phase-2/stage-a/20261106_Stage-A-Chapters.html` as built at 20:16. Paths are relative to `oath-and-action-v6-brand-guide/`. My renders, crops and measuring scripts stayed in the session scratchpad; the only file I wrote in the repository is this report.

**How I worked.** I rendered the page in Chromium with Playwright at 1440 and 390 px, captured all 234 plates at both widths, and looked at every chapter as contact sheets, with 2x crops where detail matters (rule heads, the tagline, the hinge divider, Rules 03.4, 04.1, 04.6, 08.5 and 08.7, and the proofs). Then I measured the rendered page by script: one-word last lines, characters per line, the gaps around every rule head at 1440, 1024 and 768 px, touching letter pairs at each display tracking, the shell's edges against its own column grid, every font size inside specimens against the type scale, straight quotation marks, hyphens set in tabular figures, the rule foot's columns, and which plates scroll at which width. The rubric's Craft criterion asks that "optical spacing, alignment, type detail and drawing quality hold up at 2x zoom"; I held each chapter to that and to the guide's own Rules 06.2 to 06.8 and 07.1 to 07.4.

## 1. Score

**Craft: 3 of 5.** The shell is calm and consistent, chapters 05 to 07 are measured to the pixel and the identity drawings are exact, but the display tracking fuses letters in the locked tagline and every heading, a third of the rule heads float away from their text, the hinge divider appears in three geometries, photograph placeholders remain, and the guide breaks its own scale, measure and grid rules on its own page.

## 2. Must fix

**C-M1. Photograph placeholders stand in for finished specimens (chapter 01; Rules 01.6 and 01.8; `s-01-6-correct`, `s-01-8-correct`, `s-01-8-failure`).** Three plates show a hatched grey box labeled “Photograph placeholder”. Rule 01.6's caption admits it: “in a photograph still to be made.” Master Brief section 6 defines world-class as “No lorem ipsum, no ‘TBD,’ no grey boxes.” Rule 01.8 teaches captioning, and a reader cannot judge a caption against an empty box. *Fix:* place real photographs logged in the rights ledger (federal government photographs of a Senate or a county board meeting are public domain), cropped and captioned as the rules say. If none can be cleared before the fix pass, move both rules to chapter 13 with a cross-reference and leave no box on the page.

**C-M2. The hinge divider appears in three geometries, and Rule 04.4 points builders at the odd one (chapter 04, Rule 04.4; the shell; chapters 01, 02 and 05).** The shell draws a 1 px line across the whole 808 px plate column with B 176 px from its left end, where the text column starts (`.hinge-line--a`, `guide-shell.css` line 228). Rule 04.4's own correct plate (`s-04-4-correct`), the tracker card in `s-01-1-correct`, the pieces in `s-01-pieces` and all 21 before and after pairs from Board 5 (`s-02-1-pair` to `s-02-21-pair`) center B on the line. The phone pages in `s-05-1-correct` and `s-05-3-correct` put B near the left end, as the shell does. Rule 04.4's specifications give the line, B's size and 12 px on each side, but never where B sits along the line, and its builders note says “copy its markup from any rule above”, which sends a producer to the geometry the chapter's own plate does not use. The page repeats the shell version 99 times, so a reader learns that one. *Fix:* choose one geometry (see question Q1), write it into 04.4's specifications (where B sits, how long the line runs and what it spans), and make the shell and every specimen match it.

**C-M3. Display tracking fuses letters in the locked tagline, the chapter titles and every rule title (chapter 06, Rule 06.4; tokens `oa.type.title`, `oa.type.section`, `oa.type.h3`; cover and shell).** I rendered each adjacent letter pair in Inter Tight 800 at the token's size and tracking, with kerning, and measured the white between them. At the cover's 110 px and −4.5 percent, 6 of the tagline's 24 pairs touch or overlap: “co”, “ry” (3 px overlap), “rs”, “it”, “pe” and “eo”. At the phone size, 56 px, 9 of 24 touch. At the font's own spacing none touch. Chapter and part titles at 80 px and −4 percent touch on 14 of 64 pairs (“rp”, “po” and “se” in “Purpose”; “ty”, overlapping 2 px, “yp” and “pe” in “Type”; “Fo”, “ti” and “ts” in “Foundations”). In a sample of four rule titles at 32 px and −3 percent, 31 of 113 pairs touch against 7 at the font's own spacing, and their word space measures 5.04 px, 0.157 em. Rule 06.4's own failure plate calls 0.142 em (3.4 px at 24 px) a word space where “the words begin to run together” and its correct plate shows 0.167 em, so all 99 rule titles sit nearer the guide's failure than its correct case. The tagline is the brand's principal line and the first thing on the cover; at 2x its bowls run into each other. A producer who copies the tokens copies the fault into every application. *Fix:* re-cut the tracking scale and re-measure 06.4's two plates. Measured candidates: title −3 percent (2 of 24 tagline pairs touch, “ry” and “rs”), section −2.5 percent (2 of 64), H3 −1 percent (12 of 113, all pairs with “r”, whose arm touches its neighbor even at 0), H4 −0.5 percent, so tracking still tightens as size grows. See question Q2.

**C-M4. One rule title in three floats 62 px above its own text (the shell; every chapter; Rule 07.4).** From 768 px up, the h3 is a subgrid row that holds the Rule ID column, “RULE” at 13 px over “03.1” at 32 px. That column is taller than a one-line title, so the title rides at the top of a tall row. Measured at 1440 px, 35 of 99 rule titles are one line; each sits 22 px below the rule's top hairline and 62 px above its rule text, while two- and three-line titles sit 26 to 27 px above theirs. At 1024 px 20 rules float, at 768 px 32. Rule 07.4 says “A heading sits closer to the text it introduces than to the block above,” and the guide breaks it on a third of its own rules, with an uneven rhythm down every chapter. *Fix:* make the Rule ID one line tall, with “03.1” on the title's first baseline and the word “Rule” hung above it in the margin (or set inline as at 390 px), so every title sits 24 px (`--oa-space-300`) above its text whatever its length. Re-measure with the same script at 1440, 1024 and 768 px.

**C-M5. Rule 02.22's house rules table sets H08's grades in a 60 px column (chapter 02, Rule 02.22, `s-02-22-correct`).** At 1440 px the H08 row nests its grades table (Documented, Well supported, Plausible, Contested, Hypothesis) inside the “What it asks” column, and the definitions wrap one to three words a line: “An event / or fact / on the / public / record,”. The row runs 1,939 px tall, and the plate 4,246 px. A writer who opens 02.22 to choose a title verb for a grade cannot read the table that tells them. The phone layout reads better (274 px column), so the desktop layout is the broken one. The same plate also sets its example column in tabular figures, which widens each hyphen: “three - month”, “Self - reported”, “safe - seat” (see C-S4). *Fix:* take the grades out of the cell and set them as their own full-width table under the H08 row, five rows by grade, verb and example, with tabular figures only on numbers.

**C-M6. The Gate 2 proofs never sit side by side (appendix; `s-proofs-*`; `CLAUDE.md` gate rule 4).** Gate rule 4 asks for “the original and the proposal side by side at actual sizes before asking,” and DR-71 rests the width decision on these proofs. At 1440 px the appendix column is 1,080 px and each plate adds 96 px of mat, so every pair wraps: the avatars (480 + 480), the emails (600 + 600), the letterheads (816), the social cards (1,200) and the mastheads (1,440). Each width sits in its own frame, between 412 and 1,328 px below its partner, and the masthead and social card frames scroll separately, so neither pair can be seen whole together. Only the glance plate (`s-proofs-glance`, 960 px) puts both widths in one view. *Fix:* build each pair as one comparison stage. Avatars and emails go side by side: drop the rail for the appendix at 1440 px (1,360 px available) and cut the mat padding to 24 px. Mastheads and social cards go in one frame, one above the other on a shared left edge and baseline, with one scroll for both. The shell notes' later “fit to this window” switch belongs here now, beside true size, for the wide pieces.

**C-M7. Two of the three makers set their correct applications off the type scale (chapters 01 to 04 and 08; Rule 06.2).** Rule 06.2 says “Set text in the fourteen styles of the scale, with no sizes between them,” and gives H3 as 26 to 32 px and the body as 17 px. Chapters 05 to 07 hold to it. Chapters 01 to 04 and 08 do not, in correct plates as well as failures: hinge headlines at 40 px (`s-01-18-correct` to `s-01-23-correct`, `s-02-27-correct`), 36 px with a 16 px deck (`s-04-2-correct`), 64 and 40 px (`s-04-3-correct`), 46 px (`s-04-8-correct`), 34 px (`s-08-8-correct`), 28 px card titles (`s-01-pieces`, `s-01-7-correct`, `s-03-3-correct`), 72 px numerals (`s-02-group-1` to `5`), and body text at 16 and 18 px (`s-01-21-correct`, `s-01-20-correct`, `s-02-22-correct`). A producer who copies 04.2's correct card copies sizes 06.2 forbids. The root cause is a gap: the scale grows with the window, so it names no sizes for a fixed artboard such as a 480 px card, a 600 px email or a 1,200 px social card, and two makers invented their own. *Fix:* add a fixed-artboard column to 06.2 (for example the scale's phone floor, H3 26, H4 20, intro 19, body 17, for artboards up to 480 px, and the ceiling values for 1,200 px cards), reset every specimen to it, and add a check that fails any specimen font size off the scale unless the manifest marks the plate as a failure or a historical record.

## 3. Should fix

**C-S1. The guide does not sit on its own grid (the shell; Rule 07.2).** Rule 07.2 gives the 1440 px grid 12 columns of 87.3 px with 24 px gutters and 64 px margins. The shell switches to the desktop margin, 40 px, and never to the wide grid. Its main column starts at 320 px and its text at 496 px, between the grid's column starts (286.7, 398, 509.3). At 1024 px the same edges (320, 496) miss the desktop grid (282, 362.7, 500); at 768 px the text starts at 208 against a column at 214. Only 390 px lands on the grid. A developer who copies the shell copies a page that breaks 07.2. *Fix:* frame the shell on the tokens. At 90em and up: 64 px margins, the rail on columns 1 and 2, the Rule ID in column 3, text on columns 4 to 8 (533 px, the body span 07.3 gives), plates on columns 3 to 10 and the local contents on columns 11 and 12. Repeat at 1024 and 768 px.

**C-S2. The weights of the page's rules run backwards (the shell).** A rule's boundary is a 1 px hairline, a chapter opens on 2 px, each plate head is 2 px, and a failure plate's head is a 4 px bar, the heaviest line on the page. A reader scanning a chapter sees plates as the main divisions and rules as minor ones. Rule 03.1 alone draws more than a dozen horizontal lines (rule top, hinge, two plate heads, three foot blocks and seven specification rows), and the hinge divider, the one brand device in the template, is lost among them. *Fix:* 2 px on each rule's top, hairlines on plate heads, the failure marked by its “×” and label (or 2 px at most), and fewer lines in the rule foot (C-S5).

**C-S3. Captions run long and many paragraphs end on one word (the shell; Rules 06.6 and 06.8).** Captions are 14 px Inter capped at the intro measure, 41.91 rem, which gives 76 to 103 characters a line, median 91; the source lists run a median of 85. Rule 06.8 sets 60 to 75. At 1440 px I counted 26 one-word last lines in shell text and 40 at 390 px. Most are captions and “Why it works” paragraphs; the contents page ends chapter 05's line on “it.” alone (13 px wide) and chapter 06's on “scale.”, and at 390 px the contents and the part opener end chapter 01's on “piece.”. Rule 06.6 asks that body text never end on one short word. Only `.instr` and `.rule-text` carry a wrap setting; captions, intros, “Why it works” paragraphs and the contents lines carry none. *Fix:* cap captions and sources at about 32 rem; `text-wrap: pretty` on every shell paragraph, including the contents and part-opener lines; re-run the widow count.

**C-S4. Tabular figures widen hyphens in 34 runs of text (the shell and three specimens).** Inter's tabular feature gives the hyphen a figure's width, so hyphenated words read as typing errors: the shell's specification values (13 runs, such as “drug - pricing”), token values and the closer's token table (“sans - serif”, 14 runs), `s-02-22-correct` (“three - month”, “Self - reported”, “safe - seat”), `s-02-lines` (“sign - off”, “four - dot”) and `s-01-12-correct` (“Mid - decade”). The data visualization craft review named the same fault (EX-S3). *Fix:* set `tabular-nums` only on spans that hold numbers.

**C-S5. The rule foot is cramped where it holds the most detail (the shell; every rule).** At 1440 px the specifications sit in the left three fifths of the text column and split again into two columns, so each value has 163 px. Values wrap to four and five lines, and 169 of the 246 token names and file paths in rule feet break mid-name: `--oa-color-text-` / `primary-on-newsprint`, `.../wordmark/20261028` / `_Wordmark-Display-Master.svg`. The specifications block spans two grid rows, so in 28 of 97 feet the right column leaves more than 100 px empty above “For builders”, 424 px at most (Rule 03.1 shows it). *Fix:* run the specifications across the whole text column as a three-column table (label, value, token), with each token on its own unbroken line; put Related chapters and For builders in one row under it; `align-content: start`.

**C-S6. True size is claimed unevenly, and once wrongly (all chapters; the shell's size line).** The identity designer's 68 specimens carry fixed widths and a “True size, N px wide” line. All 115 of the editor's specimens and 34 of the systems designer's carry no width: they fill the mat at whatever the window gives (712 px at 1440) and print no size line, so a reader cannot tell a 340 px phone column from a 712 px panel. Chapter 07's grids print “True size, 376 px wide” over drawings their captions call “drawn at one quarter of true size” (`s-07-2-correct`, `s-07-2-failure`, `s-07-3-correct`, `s-07-3-failure`). *Fix:* give every specimen the width of the thing it shows (a 340 px phone column, a 480 px card, a 600 px email) and print it; let the manifest carry a scale so the line reads “One quarter size”; for Rule 07.2, add a “Show the grid” switch that lays the true-size grid over the guide itself.

**C-S7. Identity plates do not reflow, and the lockup sheet is cut off on a desktop (chapters 03, 04 and 08).** At 390 px, 52 of the identity designer's 57 chapter plates scroll sideways (64 plates on the page); at 1024 px, 31 do. At 1440 px the chapter's anchor specimen, `s-03-lockups` at 932 px, loses L2, L4, L7 and L6 off its right edge (“A country tha”, “Names the pillar a piece serves, in the s”). Many of these plates are cards and pairs whose width is not the point (04.2, 04.3, 04.4, 08.4, 08.5). On phones the mat has no side padding, so specimens start at the screen edge. *Fix:* compose cards and pairs to reflow or stack under 568 px, and keep fixed widths for marks and artboards whose width is the lesson; rebuild the lockup sheet as two columns at 712 px with L6 on its own row; give the phone mat 16 px of side padding.

**C-S8. The Illustrative label comes in four forms, one of them broken (all chapters).** The shell sets it in 13 px bold capitals in a box; the editor's specimens match; the identity designer's set “Illustrative” in sentence case in a smaller box; the systems designer's write it as a plain line (“Illustrative: an invented school board…”). In `s-01-24-correct` and `s-01-24-failure` the label stretches into a full-width bordered box in regular weight that reads as an empty text field. `s-01-22-correct` carries seven labels in one card, and together with the basis box and token chips the page has more boxed labels than any other device. *Fix:* one label component from the shell, never stretched, and the density rule in question Q3.

**C-S9. Straight apostrophes on the page (Rule 06.7).** The contents and the part opener print “Write the way `you'd` explain” from the fallback list in `work/build/scripts/phase-2/guide_build.py` line 68, while chapter 02 itself prints the curly form. The identity designer's specimens and the proofs print straight marks in `s-03-lockups` (“`surface's` ink”), `s-03-minimums` (4), `s-03-misuse` (“`app's`”, “`partner's` blue”), `s-04-1-failure`, `s-08-1-failure`, `s-08-combinations` (“`view's` red”, “`pillar's` corner”), the proof labels (“`Today's` width”) and the body of both letterheads (“Planning `Board's`”, “`office's` copy”). Rule 06.7: “Use curly quotation marks and apostrophes, never straight ones.” *Fix:* run the identity build's strings through the editor's quote helper, and have the build read instruction lines from the chapter files.

**C-S10. The cover and masthead repeat the name and break the pillars (the shell).** The cover sets “Oath & Action Brand Guide, Edition 06” in Inter Tight 800, with the font's ampersand, 48 px under the wordmark with B: the near-twin names Rule 03.1's failure warns about (“a reader who meets two slightly different names starts to wonder which one is ours”). The first view shows the wordmark twice and “Brand Guide” and “Edition 06” three times (masthead, folio, h1). At 390 px the pillars break as “Explain → Track → Connect →” over “Act”. The phone masthead draws the 16 px master, 152 px wide, while Rule 03.6's correct plate and the masthead proof set the phone bar from the 24 px master. On every desktop view the rail breaks “The Ampersand and the” over “hinge”; at 390 px chapter 04's title breaks “04 The / Ampersand / and the hinge”. *Fix:* show “Brand Guide, Edition 06” as the cover's h1 and keep the full name for screen readers in hidden text; drop the folio's repeat; keep each arrow with the word after it; use the 24 px master on phones (or change 03.6 to match); shorten the rail label or widen the rail; put the chapter number on its own line under 48em.

**C-S11. Chapter 02's group openers are set as specimens (chapter 02; `s-02-group-1` to `s-02-group-5`, `s-02-index`).** Each group opener is a plate on the mat with a 72 px numeral and a title, labeled “Writing rules, group 1 of 5”. On the page it looks like a sample of something; as an image to assistive technology it carries no heading. *Fix:* a shell block for a rule group (a hairline, the group number and title in the H4 style, its one line) outside any mat, and the index as page text.

**C-S12. The hinge-circle badge reads as ® at text size (chapter 04, Rule 04.6; `s-04-6-correct`, `s-03-4-correct`).** As the end mark after “published.”, a red ring with B inside at 16 px reads as the registered trademark sign; the minimum-size plate shows the same. *Fix:* set the end mark beside ® at 16 px and look; if it reads as a trademark, end articles with B alone at cap height and keep the badge for 48 px and up.

**C-S13. Alignment inside the identity sheets (`s-03-4-correct`, `s-04-1-correct`).** In the minimum-size plate the first row aligns the tops of its boxes, so L3's wordmark sits about 25 px above L1's and L8's. In 04.1 the 24 and 16 px masters sit 2.5 px below the display masters' baseline. The “16 px master, enlarged four times” draws the vector outline over a grid, which does not show pixel fit. *Fix:* set each row of marks on one baseline; show the 16 px raster at 400 percent with nearest-neighbor scaling beside the outline.

**C-S14. The social card proof breaks the hinge headline inside its noun (`s-proofs-social-today`, `s-proofs-social-condensed`).** It reads “The zoning / code & the rent.”, while 04.2's own card breaks “The zoning code / & the rent.” at the hinge. *Fix:* break at the hinge in both proofs, so the comparison judges the wordmark and nothing else.

**C-S15. Rule titles take two grammatical forms (all chapters).** Chapters 02, 03, 04 and 08 write instructions (“Place the wordmark from its master file.”); 05 to 07 write statements (“Newsprint covers most of every page.”); 01 mixes both. In the local contents and the phone menu the two forms alternate. *Fix:* one form for all 99, the instruction, which matches the chapters' instruction lines.

**C-S16. Small alignments (the shell).** At 1440 px the local contents' top rule sits 40 px below the first plate's rule in chapter 01 (686 against 646 px) and lines up with nothing. On the contents page the two part labels sit 3 px apart, because one is a link with a 24 px target and the other plain text. *Fix:* align the local contents' rule with the chapter head's top rule, and give both part labels the same box.

## 4. Questions for Dustin

**Q1. Where does B sit on the hinge divider?** Board 5, which you approved with the writing section, centers B on the line, and so do most cards in chapters 01 to 04. The guide's shell puts B on the edge of the text column instead. *Recommendation:* center B on a line that spans the text it divides, as Board 5 does; it changes one rule in the shell rather than some 30 specimens.

**Q2. May the studio loosen the display tracking?** The tracking values came through Gate 1 with the type scale, and they make letters touch in the tagline and the headings (C-M3). *Recommendation:* approve title −3 percent, section −2.5 percent, H3 −1 percent and H4 −0.5 percent, with Rule 06.4's plates re-measured.

**Q3. How many Illustrative labels does an invented piece need?** House rule H03 puts the label beside each invented item, and some cards now carry seven. *Recommendation:* one label at the head of a piece when everything in it is invented, and a label beside each item only where a piece mixes real and invented items.
