**Spacing.** Every join in every stack holds the white the wordmark holds beside B, measured the way change C-007 set the hinge: white as an area, sliced into strips, each strip counted to 0.15x past the nearest point and averaged. My measure reproduces the master's own figures (H to B 0.203x, B to A 0.203x, nearest ink 0.093x and 0.070x). Beside B, each stack keeps the master's own placement. Between lines the strips run upright and the lower line moves until its white is 0.203x. Today's L2 already holds 0.195x between its lines, so the leading changes by less than one hundredth of a cap height.

**Small sizes.** Each stack also has a 24 px drawing (22 px capitals) and a 16 px drawing (14 px capitals), cut from the pixel wordmark masters and B's pixel masters, moved in whole pixels, with line white matched to that master's own hinge (0.238x and 0.295x) to within half a pixel. At 14 px capitals the crisp share, the part of the ink that lands on whole pixels, is 0.69 for S1 to S5 and 0.68 for S6, against 0.58 for L2 at its 90 px minimum. The guide passes 0.60.

**Files.** 18 SVG files beside the board, one display, one 24 px and one 16 px drawing per option, named `20261107_Stacked-S<n>-<Name>[-24px|-16px].svg`, each on `currentColor` with B on `--amp`, as the masters are. Each title says it is a Board 6 exploration and not an approved master. Every number the board prints is in `20261107_Board-6-Stacked-Measurements.json`.

## Recommendation

**S1, ampersand ends line one: OATH & over ACTION, flush left.**

- It is the wordmark split after B. OATH, B and the white between H and B are exactly as the master draws them, so a reader who knows the masthead knows the stack at once.
- It reads in the name's order with B at the size of the capitals. L2 sets B after both words, so a reader takes it in as OATH, ACTION, then B, and at 1.28 times the capitals. That reading order may be part of what Dustin disliked.
- It packs the name into the smallest box of the six. It stands as tall as L2 and 1.29 cap heights narrower, gives the largest capitals in a round avatar (49 px against L2's 43 px) and keeps 14 px capitals in tiles down to 115 px.
- Its leading matches the hinge and sits within 0.01x of L2's, so the block keeps L2's density.
- At its minimum it is the 16 px wordmark master on whole pixels, 79 px wide, crisp share 0.69.

If Dustin would rather set the name on a center line, S4 is the pick in its place. I recommend shipping one stacked lockup. S6 is on the board on purpose: it keeps a larger B but centers it, so his reaction tells us whether L2's size or its offset was the trouble.

Nothing in the guide changes until Dustin picks by name. A new stacked lockup changes identity geometry (gate rule 4), so L2 stays in chapter 03 until then.
