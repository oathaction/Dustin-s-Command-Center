# Creative director notes on the stage 1 icon drawings

October 28, 2026. Creative director pass, run as a subagent. It reviews the ten display masters in each of the three round-two concepts, in `work/boards/phase-2/icons-r2/`, and folds in the blind name test (`work/handoffs/phase-2/20261028_Icon-Blind-Name-Test.md`). No drawing or board changed.

How I looked: I rendered all 30 SVGs myself in Chromium at device scale 2, at 96, 48 and 24 px, on Newsprint and on Charcoal, and again with the red set to ink. I measured each drawing on a 384 px canvas: ink as a share of the field, red as a share of the painted area (ink and red together), the share of red that sits under ink, the center of mass and the bounding box in grid units. The numbers below come from that script, saved in the scratchpad. The name test had one agent rater and no people (Q-13).

Each concept section below is written to its designer and can be pasted as it stands.

## Rulings for all three

1. **All three go on to stage 2.** Each has fixable failures, and Dustin asked for three. A concept stops after stage 2 if more than two of its ten icons still fail the name test, or if any pillar mark fails the cover-the-red test or the 2x craft test.
2. **Three subjects fail in every concept**, so each concept gets a new direction for them:
   - **Campaign money becomes a banknote.** A hand with a coin read as a bed, a faucet and the numeral 2. A landscape note with a center disc names money at 24 px in any drawing language, and it stays clear of Ballot's paper and Bill's page.
   - **Feedback loop loses its half-turn symmetry.** Reload and sync glyphs are two arrows that map onto each other under a half turn, and all three loops did the same. Put two nodes on the center line, join them with a straight forward link and its arrowhead above, and bring the return back as an arc with its arrowhead below. The return is the feedback, so the red goes on it.
   - **Explain's lifted quarter reads as a lid on a hinge.** It read as a notification badge in two concepts and as crop in the third. The lifted piece stays close to the gap it left and turns on the corner it shares with the square, the way a lid opens. Each concept's note says how. If Explain still fails the name test after stage 2, its skeleton goes to Dustin as a question, with these drawings beside the approved one.
3. **No red stands alone in the badge corner.** No red shape sits by itself in the top-right 6 by 6 units of the field, where interfaces put notification badges.
4. **Straight edges stay inside the live area, 2 to 22.** Round forms may overshoot by 0.25 units at most. Overprint's Act floor line (1.75 to 22.25) and Campaign money's cuff (down to 22.25) break this today.
5. **No house in the utility set.** Housing passes everywhere, and the house is also the interface's Home glyph. Home navigation uses the wordmark, so the topic keeps the house. This goes in the decision log.
6. **Stage 2 delivers:** the redrawn display masters, hinted 16 and 24 px masters for all ten, then the name test again on all 30 with a fresh rater, and tests 2 to 4.
7. **For Dustin's named approval:** Act's full lintel in Moving part and Public sign (round one's IC-04), and any change to Explain's skeleton.

## Concept 1, Overprint

### Rulings on your calls

1. **Act keeps the frame over the opening only, with no lintel, and adds a floor line.** Approved. The floor line makes it a doorway. Condition: the hinted 16 px master scores under 0.60 against Inter Tight 800 "01" and the letter n. If it does not, add the lintel and it joins the approval list.
2. **Ballot's red covers the oval to its outer edge like a pen mark.** Approved. It is the one literal dot in the family, and the spill past the outline reads as a hand mark. Ballot still fails the name test for other reasons (note 5).
3. **The fixed offset pushes Connect's red under the left half's edge.** Overruled. Red leaning into one half marks a side, which Rule 01.1 bars. Connect is the one exception to the offset: its plane centers on the gap and prints 1.5 units straight down, at least 1 unit clear of both halves. Label the exception on the board.
4. **Track's node sits on the middle stage's nose.** Approved. That is the joint between stages, where Direction A puts the node.

### Spec changes

- Your plane rule replaces the brief's primitive list. The plane fills one shape of the drawing and prints 1.5 units left and 1.5 units down, the way a second ink misregisters, so every red belongs to a drawn part.
- The red ceiling stays at 25 percent of the painted area. Five icons sit over it or on it: Feedback loop 37, Explain 31, Track 30, Housing 26, Testify 25. Most come back as the ink grows in the notes below.

### Drawing notes for stage 2

1. **Explain.** The red on the lifted piece reads as a notification badge at 24 px. Move the plane to the inside the opening reveals: a rounded square about 6 units across in the open quarter of the main square, printed with the standard offset so it slides toward the node. The lifted piece stays an ink outline. Lift it 1 unit and turn it about 15 degrees on the corner nearest the gap, so it reads as a lid. The node sits at y 14 while the other three pillar nodes sit on y 12. Shrink the main square and the lid by about 1 unit each and bring the node to y 12.
2. **Track.** The stair spans only y 7.25 to 20.75 and carries 15 percent ink, so red reaches 30 percent and the center of mass sits about 13 percent of the field low and right. Draw three full stages of equal rise and run, from the bottom left of the landscape keyline to its top right, y 4 to 20. Keep the plane in the top stage.
3. **Connect.** Apply ruling 3. The rater read the red as "live" or "record", and a plane centered in the gap and clear of both halves reads as ground between them. Keep the halves as drawn.
4. **Act.** The 5 by 6.5 unit plane reads as an object on the floor. Keep it 5 units wide and raise its top to the center line, so the lower half of the doorway reads as lit. Pull the floor line in to x 2 to 22.
5. **Ballot.** The rater saw "a box with a frame holding a pill", then a printer: a wide shallow box with the paper standing on it. Make the box about 16 by 11 units, draw the slot as a line across its top face, and sink the paper's lower third into the slot. Enlarge the oval to about 7 by 4.5 units so it reads as a ballot oval at 24 px.
6. **Bill.** Passes. Keep the red highlight at least 2 units above the page's bottom edge so it never reads as a footer bar.
7. **Testify.** At 24 px the red disc reads as a map pin. Let the plane fill the microphone's own capsule. Raise the capsule to mouth height and angle it toward the face on a short gooseneck.
8. **Housing.** Passes. Narrow the door plane by 1 unit to bring red under 25 percent.
9. **Campaign money.** It read as a faucet. Draw a banknote: a landscape rounded rectangle about 18 by 11 units, a disc 5 units across at its center and a short bar in two opposite corners. The plane fills the center disc. Keep the disc round so it never matches Ballot's oval.
10. **Feedback loop.** It read as sync, and at 24 px as an eye or a fish, with ink 32 percent under the set's median. Follow ruling 2: two ink nodes 4 units across on the center line, a straight link with its arrowhead along the top, a returning arc with its arrowhead below. The plane fills the node the return arrives at. Draw the arcs at the set's full 1.5-unit line so the ink reaches the band.

### Keep

The 1.5-unit line, round caps and joins, the 2-unit radius and the fixed offset. With the red removed, the drawings already look finished at 24 px.

## Concept 2, Moving part

### Rulings on your calls

1. **Node 3 units with Direction A's 6-unit seat.** Approved. It is Direction A's own seat, and the bite gives this concept a cut no other set has.
2. **Track's red is its top block.** Approved. The block has to sit on a stair (note 2).
3. **Act uses the full lintel.** Kept for this concept and listed for Dustin's named approval, with round one's evidence: 0.60 against "01" without it, 0.42 with it.
4. **Red runs over 22 percent.** The ceiling rises to 25 percent of the painted area for this concept, because a moving piece needs presence. Explain at 25 passes. Act at 48 and Feedback loop at 26 come down (notes 4 and 10).
5. **Campaign money's coin may read as a head.** Confirmed: the rater saw "a person reclining on a bed". Redraw it (note 9).

### Spec change

The red piece is the one piece allowed off the 90 degree grid. It may turn up to 20 degrees on its hinge, since it is the part that moves. It stays the size of the gap it left and moves no more than 2 units from it, so it reads as part of the object.

### Drawing notes for stage 2

1. **Explain.** A dark square with a red square at its corner reads as an app icon with a badge at 24 px. Keep the red piece the size of the notch, move it no more than 2 units out, and turn it about 20 degrees on the corner nearest the node, like a lid lifting. Keep the 1.5-unit cut along the hinge side.
2. **Track.** The rater read "a platform". The full-width base slab causes it, and the center of mass sits about 16 percent of the field low and right. Build three stages of equal rise and run, climbing from bottom left to top right, y 4 to 20 on the landscape keyline. Seat the node at the joint between the first and second stages and make the red block the third.
3. **Connect.** The red square with its knock-out reads as a record button. Make the bridge a horizontal bar about 3 units tall that spans the gap edge to edge across the cut, keep the node knocked out at its center, and narrow the gap between the halves to about 5 units.
4. **Act.** Red covers 48 percent, and a red door reads as an exit sign. Narrow the leaf to about 4.5 units at its free edge, the way a door swung well open looks from the front, and add a 2-unit threshold bar in Charcoal under the opening. Red should land near 25 percent, and the Charcoal then carries the door's structure.
5. **Ballot.** Passes, the only concept where it does, with printer and ATM as second reads. Both come from the slot cut into the box's front face. Move the slot to the box's top edge, sink the paper's lower third behind it, and make the knock-out an oval about 4 by 2.5 units.
6. **Bill.** Passes. Keep the red fold clear of the page's top edge so it reads as a fold and never as a bookmark flag.
7. **Testify.** Passes. Move the microphone 1 unit toward the face and set its head at mouth height.
8. **Housing.** Passes. Keep it as drawn.
9. **Campaign money.** Follow ruling 2. Draw a Charcoal landscape note with its center disc cut out, and a second note in red sliding up and out from behind it across the cut, turned up to 20 degrees. The red note is the money changing hands.
10. **Feedback loop.** It read as reload or sync. Put the two nodes on the center line, make the forward link a straight Charcoal bar with a solid arrowhead above, and make the return a red arc with its arrowhead below. Red should fall under 25 percent once only the return is red.

### Keep

The kit, the 1-unit outer radius, the 1.5-unit cut and the even weight: the painted area runs 24 to 31 percent of the field at 96 px, which round one's flat drawings never reached.

## Concept 3, Public sign

### Rulings on your calls

1. **Explain's lifted quarter became a red swing arc with no ink lid.** Overruled. It removes part of an approved skeleton, and the rater still read crop or screenshot. Bring the lid back in ink (note 1). The 12 percent red is accepted under the new ceiling.
2. **Ballot's red is the check.** Approved. A check is a mark a voter makes, and red on the solid paper measures 4.49:1. Its drawing reads as a heart (note 5).
3. **Act's node is the latch on the jamb.** Approved. It sits on the center line where the door meets its frame.
4. **Red touches ink on Connect and Ballot.** Approved, and it becomes the concept's rule: red touches ink only where the verb acts on the object.
5. **Solid arrowheads.** Approved. A lone triangle reads as a mouse pointer, so every arrowhead gets a shaft (note 2).
6. **Feedback loop may read as sync.** Confirmed by the test (note 10).
7. **Testify's microphone may read as a lamp.** The rater saw "a person sitting at a desk" (note 7).

### Spec change

The red ceiling rises from 10 to 14 percent of the painted area. At 10 percent every verb shortens to a dash, and the swings in Explain and Act and the motion in Campaign money read as red dashes floating near the drawing.

### Drawing notes for stage 2

1. **Explain.** Draw the missing corner as an ink bar L, the same bar as the frame, lifted on its hinge at the end of the top bar and turned up 45 degrees. Shrink the frame to make room. The red arc traces the lid's swing and runs long enough to read as a quarter circle.
2. **Track.** Give the red arrow a shaft: a 45 degree bar along the line of the stair, ending in the solid head. The lone triangle reads as a cursor.
3. **Connect.** A disc with a bar across its middle reads as the no-entry sign, a Pokeball and the letter theta. Draw the halves as two bar arcs, the round-ended bar of the rest of the set, held 4 units apart. The red bridge spans the gap and the node sits on it.
4. **Act.** At 24 px it reads as the letter n. Add a floor bar under the opening, and draw the red swing as a true quarter circle from the leaf's free edge down to the floor, the path a floor plan draws for a door.
5. **Ballot.** The rater saw "a tablet with a red heart in a base". Make the box taller, with a slot bar across its top, and sink the paper's lower third into it. Redraw the check with a long right arm at 45 degrees, a short left arm and an open angle, at least 7 units across.
6. **Bill.** Also read as a SIM card, from the clipped corner and the rounded blocks. Draw the corner as a fold, a diagonal with its small triangle, and the text as two bars of different lengths above the red signature line.
7. **Testify.** Stand the figure up at a lectern: head, body bar, a lectern block at chest height, a gooseneck microphone from the lectern to the mouth, and one red sound arc ahead of the face.
8. **Housing.** Passes. Keep it as drawn.
9. **Campaign money.** It read as a 2, or an L and an o. Follow ruling 2: a bar frame about 18 by 11 units with a solid disc at its center. The red verb is an arrow with a shaft leaving the note's upper right edge, the money moving on.
10. **Feedback loop.** Follow ruling 2: two nodes on the center line, a straight forward link with its arrowhead above, a returning arc below. The red verb is the return arrow.

### Keep

The bar with round ends, the 1.5-unit gaps and the gap between head and body. The rater found this sheet the easiest to read and matched its pillar marks most confidently.

## Expected winner

Overprint, with Moving part second. At 2x, Overprint is the only sheet that already looks finished at 96 px, and its line drawings pass for premium at 24 px with the red removed. Its failures are subject drawings, and every one has a direction above. Public sign carries the strongest evidence for legibility, since the rater read it most easily, and its weakness is craft: at 96 px the bars look blunt and the verbs float as dashes. Moving part has the most even weight and the only passing Ballot, and its four failures (the badge, the platform, the record button, the exit sign) are all fixable. If Overprint still fails the name test on more than two icons after stage 2 while Public sign passes, the order changes and I will say so.
