# Apply pass red team: craft and accessibility

Oath & Action Brand Guide, Edition 06. Phase 2, November 7, 2026. The craft and accessibility lens of `work/handoffs/phase-2/20261107_Apply-Pass-Review-Brief.md`, written by one reviewer working as the design director (`.claude/agents/oa-design-director.md`) in the reviewer's seat, with the UX and accessibility lead's checks (`.claude/agents/oa-ux-accessibility-lead.md`). I reviewed `work/boards/phase-2/stage-a/20261106_Stage-A-Chapters.html` as built at commit `c6106e6` and compared it with the page at `3130c5b` where that helped. Paths are relative to `oath-and-action-v6-brand-guide/`. My scripts, renders and crops stayed in my session scratchpad; this report is the only file I wrote in the repository. I did not read the other two reviewers' reports.

**How I worked.** I read the apply pass brief, the decision log from DR-93 to D2-414, the four makers' notes and both agent files. I rendered the page in Chromium with Playwright at 1440, 1024, 390, 360 and 320 px. I looked at chapters 08 and 11 whole at 1440 px, and at every new or changed plate in chapters 01, 03, 04, 08 and 11 and the proofs at twice size, with the step rows also at 1x enlarged three times in color and in grayscale. Then I measured by script: step-row geometry against the text edge, which plates scroll at which width, what the Fit to window switch does to type size, ink coverage of each lockmark master at the size its place uses, the social card's sign-off when a feed shrinks the card, the end mark's size and spacing, which S1 master each drawing uses, text contrast inside the plates, accessible names, and a keyboard pass through chapters 08 and 11.

## 1. Verdict

Most of the new work would pass a leading studio's desk. S1 is the Board 6 drawing to the byte and sits on whole pixels at its small masters. Chapter 11's marks read by shape alone on both grounds and in grayscale, every plate names each state in words for a screen reader, and keyboard focus stays visible and clear of the sticky masthead. Four things would stop that studio. At the guide's main desktop width the plate that shows DR-128 hides step 6. The lockmark's four places draw what reads as two marks of different weight. The sign-off on a social card falls to Rule 08.1's own failure once a feed shrinks the card. And the H.R. 7008 card is drawn on a different geometry from the chapter it borrows its marks from.

## 2. Must fix

**CA-M1. Rule 11.1's correct plate hides step 6 at 1440 px, and Fit to window shrinks it below the chapter's own floor (chapter 11; `s-11-1-correct`, with `s-11-list-1024-newsprint`, `s-11-list-1024-charcoal` and `s-11-page-1024-newsprint`).** The plate exists to show that step 6 reads “Governor” on a state bill and “President” on a federal one. At 1440 px the plate column is 867 px and the plate, with its mat, 1,120 px. True size is the default, so the frame shows steps 1 to 4 and half of step 5; both step 6 names sit off-screen to the right. Fit to window scales the plate to 0.753, and its 13 px step names and label render at 9.8 px. Rule 11.7 sets 13 px as the floor, and its failure plate is a tracker with 9 px numbers. At a 1024 px window the scale is 0.59, which renders 7.7 px names, and at 390 px it is 0.35, which renders 4.5 px. No state of the switch shows the lesson at a readable size on the width Dustin is most likely to open. The three 1,024 px extras after Rules 11.5 and 11.6 do the same, though their lessons survive the crop better. *Fix:* redraw Rule 11.1's correct plate as two 360 px story cards side by side, 720 px with their gap, each with its six-name key, so “Governor” and “President” sit beside each other at true size inside the 867 px column. For the 1,024 px extras, let a plate wider than the column break out to the wide frame the proofs appendix already uses, 1,312 px at 1440, so they show whole at true size from about 1,200 px. Keep Fit to window for narrower screens.

## 3. Should fix

**CA-S1. The lockmark's four places draw two marks of different weight (chapter 08, Rule 08.2, `s-08-2-correct`; also `s-03-lockups` and `s-03-4-correct`).** Rule 08.3's specification says “The same in every place: nine nodes, their links and the red lever.” I rendered each master at the size its place uses and measured how much of the field is ink. The 24 px master in the page header and footer covers 33.6 percent of its field, with 29 percent near-black pixels and a lever of 37 red pixels. The display master at 48 px in the sign-off covers 7.2 percent, with 1.6 percent near-black and a lever of 11 red pixels; at 52 px in the masthead, 6.8 percent. The display master's links also fall between pixels at 48 and 52 px and render as two-pixel grey smears. On the places plate the page header and footer show a dense block of dots, and the masthead and sign-off show a hairline square with a three-pixel red dot. The lever at 24 px carries three times the red of the lever at twice the size. The same split shows in chapter 03, where L3 is a fine grid on the lockup sheet and a black dot cluster on the minimum sheet. *Fix:* this is identity geometry, so it needs Dustin's named approval under gate rule 4. Put it on the changes board as a question: a pixel-fit 48 px master for the sign-off and masthead, with nodes and lever between today's two drawings, shown beside today's at true size in all four places. If he keeps today's masters, Rule 08.1 should say in words that the small masters are drawn heavier so the drawing holds at 16 and 24 px, and Rule 08.3's “the same” should name what stays the same.

**CA-S2. The sign-off on a social card becomes Rule 08.1's failure once a feed shrinks the card (chapter 08, Rule 08.2; `s-proofs-social`; D2-327).** A 1,200 by 630 card shows in most feeds at 500 px wide or less. I rendered the proof card at 1x and reduced it as a platform would. At 500 px the 48 px sign-off becomes a 20 px lockmark drawn from the display master, with no near-black pixel and a lever of three red pixels; at 360 px it is a grey smudge. Rule 08.1's failure plate describes the same thing: “Its nodes shrink to the width of a link and the lever turns into a single pink pixel.” D2-327 chose the display master because the creative director found the 24 px master soft at 40 px on Board 6. The notes record no test of either master at the size a feed shows the card. *Fix:* choose the sign-off's master by the size the card is seen at, test it at 500 and 360 px previews before choosing, and write that test into Rule 08.2's specifications. CA-S1's 48 px master, if Dustin approves one, should pass this test too.

**CA-S3. The H.R. 7008 card is drawn on a different geometry from chapter 11, and its status runs past Rule 11.1's form (chapter 01, `s-01-pieces`, the Track card; chapter 11, `s-11-4-correct`).** The card imports chapter 11's drawing code, but places it differently. Its step row is 288 px, centered in the card, so the first circle sits 38 px in from the text edge at 1440 px, 31 px at 390 px and 16 px at 360 px. Every chapter 11 row is 324 px with 54 px steps, and its first circle sits 16 px in at every width. The card prints its six-name key directly under the marks; chapter 11's own story card prints no key at all, while its lists put the key after the last bill, as Rule 11.5 sets for phones. Rule 11.1's specification gives status words as “in bold, for the step the bill is at now, then one plain sentence.” The card prints that, then a second paragraph of four sentences with three dates and two vote counts, which Rule 11.6 places on a bill's own page. The Track card now runs about twice the height of the Explain card beside it, and the four pieces stop reading as a set. *Fix:* draw the card from Rule 11.4's story card: the 324 px row starting 16 px in, as chapter 11 does, with the card made 360 px wide if its column needs the room. Give both story cards the same key in the same place, and add that key to `s-11-4-correct`. Keep the slash's reason to one sentence and move the dates and counts into the source note or a bill's page. The editor and the research lead should decide which facts stay on the card; this finding is about its form.

**CA-S4. At body size the end mark stands taller than the capitals and reads as a dangling “and” (chapter 04, Rule 04.6, `s-04-6-correct`; DR-118, D2-333).** DR-118, approved by name, puts B “at the text's cap height”. Rule 04.6 adds “or 14 px tall where they are shorter”, from Rule 03.4's floor for identity marks. At 17 px Inter the capitals are about 12.4 px, so at the size nearly every article uses, the page never shows DR-118's cap height. B renders 14 by 16 px, rises 1.6 px above the capitals and over the ascenders of “l” and “h”, and carries a heavier weight than the text. It follows the period after a word space, 4.8 px measured. At true size, “minutes. &” reads as a bold ampersand opening a new clause. *Fix:* show Dustin the floor conflict on the changes board at true size, B at 12.4 px from its 16 px master beside B at 14 px, both after the same paragraph. Set the end mark after a wider fixed space, for example half an em, kept as a no-break space, so it reads as the end of the piece. Test on Newsprint and Charcoal.

**CA-S5. Rule 04.6's plates and L6's plate are assembled loosely (chapter 04, `s-04-6-correct`, `s-04-6-failure`; chapter 03, `s-03-l6`).** In the correct plate the captions “The end mark: B alone, in the text's ink, 16 px master” and “The badge at 96 px on the back of a 4 by 6 in event card, true size” touch the frame's left rule, the second also touches its bottom rule, and the event card butts the left border. L6's caption does the same, and at 1440 px L6's own lockmark sits outside the frame with no Fit to window switch. The failure plate leaves an empty 20 px band between the article box and the comparison box. Its comparison is a 1x PNG, so on any 2x screen the ring in the “enlarged twice” row shows stair steps, and its “minutes.” is set in Liberation Sans under a paragraph set in Inter. *Fix:* pad these plates as chapter 11 pads its own, 16 to 24 px. Give L6 the switch. Export the PNG at 2x and 3x with `srcset`, or redraw the registered sign as SVG outlines from Liberation Sans. Set “minutes.” in Inter in both rows, so the marks are the only difference.

**CA-S6. Failure bands come in two builds and are missing where a plate could pass for real (chapters 04, 08 and 11; D2-308).** Chapter 08's band in `s-08-3-failure` leaves a 16 px empty strip between the band and the card's top rule; chapter 11's bands sit flush on the card. Rule 08.2's failure, a flyer with the lockmark as bullets, and Rule 04.6's failure, an article ending, could each pass for a real piece, yet neither carries the band, while Rule 08.3's does. *Fix:* one band component from the shell, flush with the card, on every failure plate that could pass for a real piece.

**CA-S7. The pillar row states a red second sign at 48 px that no plate draws, and its spacing follows boxes instead of ink (chapter 01, `s-01-pillars`; DR-111).** The caption says “from 48 px its icon also shows its red part as a second sign”. The 48 px row has no current pillar, and no plate in the page shows any pillar icon with its red part, so a producer cannot tell which part of each icon turns red. In the 48 px row each icon sits in a 48 px box, so the gap from ink to word changes with the drawing; Act's hand is narrow, and “Act” stands visibly farther from its icon than “Explain” from the book. At 24 px the hairline icons read lighter than the 17 px words beside them. *Fix:* add a 48 px row with a current pillar, its word underlined and its red part drawn, as a section opener would set it. Space each word from the icon's ink. Carry the 24 px weight to chapter 09's pixel-fit masters, the creative director's open item.

**CA-S8. The H.R. 7008 card's text alternative leaves out its sources (chapter 01, `s-01-pieces`; WCAG 2.2 SC 1.1.1; house rule H04).** The pieces figure is `role="img"` with a 1,724-character `aria-label`. It gives a screen reader the 53 votes, the 7 to 4 committee vote, the 232 to 198 House vote and their dates, and none of the card's source line; it also drops the Explain card's attribution to our systems atlas. The 2020 example (`s-01-uneven`) and Rule 02.25's row (`s-02-25-correct`) carry their sources through a description. A name that long is also hard to review word by word in several screen readers. *Fix:* make `s-01-pieces` a readable group, since all its text is real HTML, or move it to the group-and-description form the other sourced plates use, with every source line included.

**CA-S9. Chapter 08 lists two of its three masters, and the third goes by two names (chapter 08, Rules 08.1 and 08.2; chapter 03, `s-03-minimums`).** The masthead and the sign-off both draw from the display master, yet both rules' Files list only the 16 and 24 px masters, and the builders' note says “Ask the editor for the display master's file.” The file is in the package at `work/build/assets/identity/lockmark/20261028_Lockmark-Master-V5.svg`. The minimum table calls it “the fifth edition's master”; chapter 08 calls it “the display master”. *Fix:* list the file in both rules under one name, “display master”, and rename the file when the asset package does (D2-298).

**CA-S10. The footer place is drawn two ways (chapter 08, `s-08-2-correct`; appendix, `s-proofs-letterhead`).** Rule 08.2's plate sets the 24 px lockmark directly before the tagline at the left of the footer. The letterhead proof sets the tagline at the left and the lockmark at the far right of the footer rule. Rule 08.2 says “24 px beside the tagline”, and its reason is that “a reader finds it without looking”. *Fix:* choose one arrangement, draw both pieces to it and write it into Rule 08.2's specifications.

**CA-S11. The lockup sheet's first row does not line up (chapter 03, `s-03-lockups`).** S1 sits about 22 px below its cell's top rule while L1 sits about 61 px below its own, and S1's name, job and minimum run 15 px lower than L1's across the same row. *Fix:* set each row's marks on one baseline or one top, and each row's labels on one line.

**CA-S12. The kicker's topic icon is heavier in size and lighter in stroke than the kicker it sits in (chapter 01, Rule 01.21, `s-01-21-correct`; D2-373).** A 24 px hairline icon beside 13 px semibold capitals adds about 6 px to the Analysis row, so its headline sits lower than the six others in the plate, and the strokes read fainter than the capitals. *Fix:* size the topic icon to the kicker, for example 16 px from a pixel-fit master centered on the cap height, so the row keeps the rhythm of the rows around it. Carry the size to chapter 10.

## 4. Notes

**CA-N1. The “never happened” mark differs between row and key (chapter 11).** In a row the slash breaks around the number into two strokes of about 5 px, which at 26 px read as ticks beside a pale ring. The key draws a whole slash through an empty ring. Draw the key from the row's mark.

**CA-N2. The take-part mark alone may read as a reload icon (chapter 11, the mark beside “You can take part now”).** A ring broken at the upper right with a dot on the break resembles common refresh and sync glyphs. Run the similarity check against chapter 11's coming utility icons before they are drawn.

**CA-N3. Step rows start 14 to 16 px in from the text edge in chapter 11's phone lists.** On phones, where the names sit in a key, hanging the first circle on the text edge would align the row with the bill name and the status words above and below it.

**CA-N4. Numbers split from their units outside chapter 11.** Rule 08.1's reason breaks “24” from “px”, the minimum table breaks “16 px master” and “7 pt” twice, the pillars caption breaks “48 px” at 390 px, and the Track card's deck breaks “As” from “of November 5, 2026” at 390 px. D2-359 fixed this in chapter 11 with no-break spaces; the identity and editor builds could use the same helper.

**CA-N5. Chapter 11's group “Trackers” is set as a paragraph.** A screen reader user moving by heading goes from the chapter title to Rule 11.1 without hearing the group. Chapters 01 and 02 share the pattern, so it is one shell decision.

**CA-N6. Chapter 11's first intro sentence restates its instruction line and changes one word.** The instruction line says “a glyph and a word”; the next sentence says “a mark and a word”. One of the two could go, and the chapter could keep one word for the thing.

**CA-N7. The scroll hint names the wrong limit.** At 1440 px, plates wider than the column print “Wider than this screen.” “Wider than this column” would be true at every width.

**CA-N8. The part opener's sub-lines align with nothing, and chapter 09 keeps its old name.** In the Symbols and graphics opener, “Coming later” and chapter 11's instruction line start 31 px in, while chapter titles start 50 px in. The opener, chapter 08's related chapters and Rule 08.3's specification still call chapter 09 “Pillar marks”, where DR-111 says pillar icons (the editor's contradiction 6, still open).

**CA-N9. Some wide plates scroll without a Fit to window switch.** At 1440 px: `s-03-l6` and `s-03-6-correct`. At 1024 px: `s-11-1-failure` and `s-08-1-correct`. At 320 px, chapter 11's 360 px plates scroll inside their frames; the page itself never scrolls sideways at 320, 360, 390, 640, 720, 768, 800 or 1024 px, and SC 1.4.10's exception for content that needs a fixed layout covers true-size specimens.

**CA-N10. Specification tables change column widths from rule to rule.** The label column is about 140 px in Rule 08.1, 250 px in Rule 08.2 and about 120 px in chapter 11, so values start at a different place down a chapter. A fixed column in the shell would hold one line.

**CA-N11. Invented plates carry the Illustrative label twice.** Every invented plate in chapters 01, 04, 08 and 11 carries one label at the head of the piece and a second in the shell's caption. DR-97 asks for one at the head. All four makers follow the same pattern, so it is one shell decision.

**CA-N12. Kicker separators differ within one plate (`s-01-21-correct`).** “Act · How to take part” sets a tight dot with both words in Charcoal; every other kicker in the plate sets a wide dot with the topic in Stone. If “How to take part” is a kind of Act piece, the plate could say so in its caption.

## 5. Do the four makers' work read as one hand?

The four makers share one label, one caption style and one plate head, and chapter 01 imports chapter 11's step marks while chapter 08 matches them, so at reading distance the page reads as one hand. Up close, the seams show in four places: the step row's placement and key (CA-S3), the failure band (CA-S6), the padding inside plates (CA-S5) and the footer place (CA-S10). Each comes from a maker drawing a shared device in their own builder, and each would close if the shell or chapter 11's builder owned the device.

## 6. Tested and not tested

### Tested

- **Renders.** Chromium through Playwright at 1440, 1024, 390, 360 and 320 px. Chapters 08 and 11 whole at 1440 px. At twice size: Rules 08.2 and 08.3, chapter 11's state key, five-bill list, story card, laws-and-stops plate, size plate and bill page, the pillars figure and the pieces figure, Rule 01.21's kickers, the lockup sheet, minimum sheet, minimum table, surfaces sheet and L6, and Rule 04.6's two plates, with the end mark also at four times. At 1x: the five proofs. The pillars figure also at 1024 and 390 px, the pieces at 390 px, and chapter 11's Rules 11.1, 11.4 and 11.6 at a 360 px window.
- **S1.** A script compared each of S1's three masters in `work/build/assets/identity/lockups/` with Board 6's files: path data and viewBox equal. Every S1 drawing on the page (11 drawings) uses the master its range sets, capitals 14 px from the 16 px master, 22 px from the 24 px master, 40, 44 and 100 px from the display master. The 16 and 24 px masters rendered at 1x and enlarged six times: stems and bars sit on whole pixels.
- **Lockmark.** Ink coverage, near-black share and red pixel count of each master at 16, 24, 48, 52 and 96 px, rendered at 1x on Newsprint.
- **Sign-off at feed size.** The 1,200 by 630 proof card rendered at 1x and reduced to 500 and 360 px wide with Lanczos resampling, the corner enlarged.
- **End mark.** Its box, gap and markup by script: 14 by 16 px, 4.8 px after the period, a no-break space before it, hidden from assistive technology.
- **Step rows.** Position and width against the text edge by script at 1440, 390 and 360 px for `s-01-pieces`, `s-08-3-correct`, `s-11-3-correct`, `s-11-4-correct` and `s-11-5-correct`. Six rows on both grounds at 1x, enlarged three times, in color and in grayscale: every state tells apart by shape, and the take-part disc stays visible on Charcoal in gray.
- **Scrolling and the switch.** Which plates scroll at five widths in chapters 01, 03, 04, 08 and 11 and the proofs. Fit to window's scale and smallest rendered text on five plates at 1440 px, one at 1024 px and three at 390 px.
- **Screen reader names, read from the DOM.** Role, name and description of 17 plates; focusable elements inside plates (only three, all inside groups); the scroll frames' roles and names; the size switch's group name and pressed states.
- **Keyboard.** Tab through chapters 08 and 11 at 1440 px (113 stops), and from chapter 08 at 390 px and chapter 11 at 360 px. Every stop shows a 2 px ring, Verdigris on Newsprint at 3.37 to 1 or Charcoal on the mat; no stop lands under the sticky masthead; no stop is invisible. Stops under 24 px are inline links in sentences or spaced lists, which SC 2.5.8's exceptions cover.
- **Contrast in context.** Every HTML text node in the new plates against its nearest painted background: none under its threshold. SVG step numbers flagged only where my script read the page's ground in place of the filled circle behind them; I checked those by eye.
- **Reflow.** No page-level sideways scroll at 320, 360, 390, 640, 720, 768, 800 and 1024 px.
- **Before and after.** The page at `3130c5b`, extracted to my scratchpad, under the same doubled root font size described below: its masthead part links ran 12 px past the edge, against 187 px now that a second part link sits there.

### Not tested

- Screen readers by ear (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, TalkBack). I read names and descriptions from the DOM only.
- A real browser text-size setting at 200 percent. I doubled the root font size at 1280 px as a stand-in: text in the plates grew with nothing clipped, and the masthead's part links ran 187 px past the edge. The shell's media queries are in em, so a real setting would likely switch to the phone menu first; I report neither result as a finding.
- Forced colors, Firefox, Safari, real phones, and print of chapters 08 and 11.
- The letterhead PDF, and the social card on a real platform; I simulated a feed's reduction.
- Color-vision simulations other than grayscale.
- Chapters 02, 05, 06 and 07, the cover, the contents and the 2020 example's content. I did not check any fact, quotation or source; that belongs to the brand and evidence lens.
- Any reader.
