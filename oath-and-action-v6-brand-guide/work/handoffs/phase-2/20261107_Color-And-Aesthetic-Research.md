## How to read this report

Every claim carries a grade from H08 in chapter 02's writing section: Documented, Well supported, Plausible, Contested or Hypothesis. A claim about what a brand or forecaster chose is Documented when I opened the brand's or forecaster's own page. "Opened" means I fetched the page and read it. "Snippet" means I read only a search result's summary. I mark trend reports as trend reports: they are vendor marketing or forecasting, useful as creative inputs and never as evidence of what readers think. Design judgments are labeled as design judgments.
