## What I made

One self-contained page that opens with the three sets side by side in the pillar row, aligned by pillar, at 48 px in one color with each word and ID beside its icon, then the same twelve at 128 px in two colors. A Prussian band gives the recommendation and one question. The page then shows how I chose from the 122, and each set in use: why each icon fits and what to watch, the sizes 16, 24, 48 and 128 px with Chrome's own 16 and 24 px rasters enlarged four times, the pillar row at 48 px with Track current and the compact row at 24 px, Connect's section opener on Charcoal, the four kickers from chapter 01's pieces at 340 px, and the rounded two-color tiles on Charcoal at 128 and 76 px. It closes with the naming test and the measurements.

Every icon is the supplied master SVG of its edition (red-black, black, red-newsprint, newsprint) or its rounded Charcoal two-color tile, inlined with its ids made unique per placement and its box set to the size shown. No path, stroke or color changed.

Explain is CL-019 in all three. No other drawing among the 122 says explain as plainly. The nearest, CL-068 Education, is the same book with a bulb and already names a topic, and CL-023 Evidence shares the magnifier with Track and the watchdog drawings.

### How I chose

All 122 drawings read on the five red-black contact sheets, then: (1) sheets 01 to 03 only, 72 drawings, since each of the 50 reform drawings stands for a proposal the atlas weighs and a pillar mark on one would look like an endorsement; (2) the 29 of those the October 29 trace review found still read at 24 px; (3) ten set aside whose subject names a harm or one policy area (CL-010, CL-013, CL-040, CL-051, CL-054, CL-057, CL-060, CL-067, CL-070) or that raters took for a refresh sign in all three earlier tests (CL-024). The 19 left are on the board with a one-line verdict each.

## Recommendation

**Keep Set A and change how it is used.** These are the only four of the 122 drawn for the pillars, so no topic or reform page shares the drawing. Sets B and C each borrow drawings that already name a topic (B three: Public Records, Household Income, Petition; C two: Civic Ties, Voting), so a reader would meet one drawing with two meanings. A's hand covers any next step, where a ballot (C) or a signed page (B) names one kind of step, and B's petition can read as advocacy. A needs no new identity approval (DR-04 stands).

Five changes in use: the word beside the icon in every row and always under 48 px (R9); one color in a row, with an underline on the current pillar's word at every size and its icon's red as a second sign from 48 px up (DR-66); at 16 px the icon is decoration beside its word; the grid corner retires and the icon takes its place in kickers and section openers (DR-104); the app icon and avatars use the rounded two-color Charcoal tile from 76 px (R13, D2-42).

Weaknesses Dustin would accept: Track is the busiest mark and the faintest at 16 px (1 pixel reaches 3:1); Act measures 63.9 against Explain's 79.4 px at 128 px, about a fifth smaller. Second choice: Set C, if he wants people and public acts. Set B ties best as a family, at the cost of three topic drawings and two pillars that both show writing.

### Naming risk

My own reading, no outside readers. Pairs at risk:

- **A:** Track and Explain (the magnifier reads as "look into"); Explain and Connect, the closest pair in shape (60th percentile of 7,381 pairs), if the rings read as "compare". Connect reads first as a Venn diagram. Act is the phone tap gesture.
- **B:** Connect and Act, since the letter and the signed page both show writing; Track and Explain, since the folder and the book both hold reading. The folder, envelope and pen-on-page are also app glyphs (files, contact us, edit). CL-061 is wide and shrinks to a strip at 24 px in a kicker.
- **C:** few confusions, but the ballot narrows Act to voting, and in all three earlier tests raters took other ballot-box drawings for printers or trays. Connect and Act are its closest pair in shape (75th percentile). CL-006 is the faintest icon on the board at 16 px (no pixel reaches 3:1).

### One question for Dustin (on the board)

R9 asks for the pillar's word beside a mark under 48 px. In a kicker the content label sits beside the icon instead: Explainer, Tracker and Act carry the pillar's word, and Guide does not name Connect. I recommend the label counts as the word in a kicker; the alternative is that the kicker keeps a topic icon and the pillar mark stays out of it. This touches the kicker definition (Rule 08.8), which section 1 also rewrites as the grid leaves the kicker, so the main session should reconcile the two.
