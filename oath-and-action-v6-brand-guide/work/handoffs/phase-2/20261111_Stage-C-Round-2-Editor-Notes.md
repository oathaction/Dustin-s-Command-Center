## What I read and how

- **The changes.** Every reader string that differs between commit `df0fb08d` (the frozen page round 2 reviewed) and HEAD (`81695fe4`) in `work/build/guide/chapters/`, `work/build/guide/specimens/`, `work/build/guide/code/` and `work/build/tokens/tokens.json`, pulled by a script in my scratch folder (`extract.py`). Each JSON file was flattened to its keys. Each specimen and code file gave its visible text, its alt, aria-label, title and data attributes that hold words, its HTML and CSS comments, and its scripts' comments and word strings, with whitespace collapsed, so CA-S2's reformat of the 48 `c-18` files counts as no change unless a word moved. 169 files changed, with 3,296 raw strings; after dropping token names, values, figures and duplicates, I read **486 unique reader strings** in 101 files. The token table's 2,525 added strings come down to 28 new ones, since its rows reprint tokens.json's descriptions, which I read there.
- **Left out of the read, since no reader sees them:** the `.keys.json` files (the guide check reads them; the build prints none) and the manifests' `notes` (the build prints a note only for a proofs specimen without a caption, and no changed specimen is one).
- **Chapter 12.** Rule 12.4's rewrite under DR-216 landed after `df0fb08d` (commit `a085f846`), so it falls in the diff. I read it with the fix pass, and its rows sit under the identity designer, whose generator writes it.
- **W3C's criterion words** in Rule 19.1's list, and call 4's grade line, I checked for facts only and left as written.
- **In place.** I looked up strings in the fixed page at `scratchpad/final/guide.html` by search only, never reading it whole.
- **Facts checked by script:** every atlas line against `work/extract/atlas-v7-data.json` (Loops R1 and R8, their grades and summaries, links L01, L38 and L39, variables A4, A5, A6 and X5 and domain D09, Reforms R-01, R-04 and R-30); every contrast figure in a changed string, from tokens.json's hex values (Pillar Red on Charcoal 4.49, Dim Newsprint on Charcoal 7.26, Newsprint on Charcoal 13.29, Stone on Charcoal 3.10, Newsprint and White on Prussian 9.42 and 13.28); tokens.css at 94,715 bytes, 95 KB; 504 tokens, 13 categories and the 17 table groups by count; chapter 19's map, 33 met, 8 in part, 9 gaps and 5 not used, with the gaps owed by chapters 02, 16, 18, 20 and 23; 68 percent for ease-out at half of 400 ms; the bills' numbers, dates and steps across chapters 11, 17 and 18; Rule 03.4's minimum for L8; WCAG 2.2's text-spacing values.
