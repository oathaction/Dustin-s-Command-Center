## Blocking

**B1. Specimens in chapters 05 to 07 touch their frame.**
- Where: 38 plates, among them the plates of Rules 05.4, 05.5, 05.7, 06.1 to 06.8, 06.10 and 07.2 to 07.5, and four readable tables: the seven colors, the color roles, the artboard sizes and the body spans.
- What: the fix pass gave every stage a 1 px Stone keyline (the accessibility report's AX-S13). The systems designer's flowing specimens carry no inner margin, so at 1,440 their text sits 0 to 2 px from the keyline. Descenders in Rule 06.6 sit on it, and the hanging quotation mark in Rule 06.1 crosses it. It shows in every board view of three chapters and fails the craft bar at 2x.
- Fix: in `guide-shell.css`, give every stage without a `width_px` an inner margin of `--oa-space-300` (24 px), and `--oa-space-200` (16 px) under 48em, inside the keyline. Leave fixed-width artboards at true size. Look again at Rules 06.1, 06.4, 06.6 and 07.4 at 2x and rerun the guide check at four widths.

**B2. Rule 04.8's crop leaves a sliver of B.**
- Where: Rule 04.8, correct application, the half-letter report cover.
- What: the crop leaves a piece of B's beak about 2 px wide and 35 px tall inside the cover's right edge, cut off from the glyph. It reads as a stray mark at true size and plainly at 2x, on the plate A4 sends Dustin to. The cover also sits inside a second frame that holds the Illustrative label, so the crop seems to stop at an inner line 12 px short of the plate's edge.
- Fix: move the crop until the beak falls wholly outside the trim or wholly inside it, and check the failure plate the same way. Put the Illustrative label in the caption, or over the cover's top margin, so the cover's own edge is the edge B bleeds off.

**B3. Phone pages scroll 2 px at 390.**
- Where: the 29 plates drawn 360 px wide, in chapters 01, 02, 03, 05, 07 and 08, among them Rules 01.1, 01.22, 01.23, 02.24, 05.1 and 05.3.
- What: the phone mat leaves a 358 px stage, so each 360 px page overflows by 2 px. The frame then prints "Wider than this screen. Scroll the frame sideways to see all of it." and becomes a focus stop. A phone reader meets 29 false prompts and 29 extra Tab stops, and the board's 390 px view of Rule 05.1 shows one.
- Fix: in the shell, let a stage that overflows its frame by 4 px or less draw into the mat's side margin, centered, with no hint and no focus stop, so the page stays at true size. Check the frame count at 390 and 360 px.

## Rulings on the editor's flags

1. **B's master ranges.** Rule 04.1 governs: the 16 px master for B 16 to 23 px tall, the 24 px master from 24 to 47 px, the display master from 48 px. Rule 04.6 and the masters' own table agree with it. Rule 04.3's specification changes to "16 px master for capitals 16 to 23 px tall, 24 px master from 24 to 47 px, display master from 48 px". It is one string in the identity designer's chapter script; make it in the blocker pass.
2. **Rule 07.6's scrolling frame.** Teach what the guide does. The builders note becomes: "A frame that must scroll is a group with its own name, such as the table's title, and takes `tabindex="0"` only while it overflows, so a keyboard user can scroll it and a page with many frames keeps its landmark list short." Also in the blocker pass.
3. **Report for Dispatch stories.** Keep Report. A Dispatch story says what the record shows about a decision; Tracker belongs to a page that follows one decision over time.
4. **Rule 01.5's map.** Keep three fills. The source line carries Florida's and Illinois's limits, and a fourth fill would teach a distinction the headline does not claim.
5. **The 360 px phone pages.** B3.
6. **The photographs.** Fetch the originals before print (still needs work).
