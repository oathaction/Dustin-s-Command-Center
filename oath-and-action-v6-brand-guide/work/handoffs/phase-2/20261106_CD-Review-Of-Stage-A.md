## Decision 13's example, reconsidered

My first draft gave chapter 01 the House vote on H.R. 7008 as its example of an uneven finding. The main session asked me to weigh two concerns. A party-line vote shows each side voting as a bloc, the same act on both sides, so it teaches DR-93 weakly: DR-93 is about the record showing one party or official doing more of something. It also echoes "Democrats voted against a stock trading ban", the framing the Lead Stories fact check flagged, even with the bill described whole. Both concerns hold, and I changed my recommendation.

Our systems atlas holds a better candidate. Issue I155, "Unsupported fraud claims weaken acceptance of defeat", is graded documented. After the 2020 election, claims of fraud large enough to change the result failed in court. Lost, Not Stolen, a 2022 review by conservative lawyers and former officials, found no evidence of outcome-changing fraud in any of 64 court cases. In one of them the Trump campaign asked the Third Circuit to stop Pennsylvania's certified result, and the court refused, noting that the campaign "never alleges that any ballot was fraudulent". That record shows one side doing more of a thing DR-93 names, a threat to the peaceful transfer of power. A writer applying the rule names who acted, uses record language at the documented grade, and adds no counterweight from the other party. The review's authors also show one standard of evidence at work, since conservatives wrote it.

A13 now recommends adopting the wording now and adding the example before Gate 2, once the research lead has read the Third Circuit's opinion and the review in full and written the source line. I155 is the named candidate. The House vote stays on the card as option (b). The research lead has not opened I155's sources; the grade is the atlas's.

Rule 02.25's last row now has its own card, A14. Its job differs from chapter 01's example. The row replaces "One party blocked the bill." with the record, and Rule 01.13 says a party-line vote is printed with its count by party. With both of the bill's provisions in the same sentence, the count gives the context the fact check said the bare charge lacked. I recommend adding it. The H10 erratum moves to A15.
