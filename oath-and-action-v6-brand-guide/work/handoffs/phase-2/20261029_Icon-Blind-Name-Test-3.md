# Icon blind name test, after the Overprint fix pass

Main session, October 28 and 29, 2026. Test 1 ("Name it") on the two concepts still in play: Overprint after its fix pass, and Moving part as finished at stage 2.

## Method

- Sheet W: Overprint's ten icons after the fix pass, unlabeled and shuffled, each at its 16 px master, its 24 px master and its display master at 96 px. Sheet X: Moving part's ten finished icons, the same sheet as stage 2's sheet T with new codes. Both in `work/boards/phase-2/icons-r2/blind-test-3/`.
- Four fresh agent raters with no project context. Rater 1 saw sheet W alone. Raters 2 to 4 saw both sheets, in different orders. Each named every icon, matched the four pillar stories, and judged the families. Full answers: `20261029_Icon-Blind-Name-Test-3-Answers.md`.
- For Moving part, the stage 2 rater's answers on sheet T are counted too, so each concept has four readings.
- Agents, not people. This stands in for the reader check (Q-13) and does not replace it.

## Results at 24 px

"Named" means the subject was the rater's first guess. "Weak" means it appeared only as a second guess or as a possible mistake.

| Subject | Overprint (4 readings) | Moving part (4 readings) |
|---|---|---|
| Housing | Named 4 | Named 4 |
| Bill | Named 4 | Named 2, weak 2 (the letter "b" or "L") |
| Feedback loop | Named 3, weak 1; recycle, sync and spinner risks | Named 0, weak 1; swap, sort, the kebab menu |
| Testify | Named 1, failed 3 (a capsule, a sign, a torch) | Named 2, weak 2 (a lever, a joystick) |
| Ballot | Named 0, weak 2; printer, inbox, stamp | Named 0, weak 4; tray, sofa, USB stick |
| Campaign money | Named 0, weak 2; camera, card, hard drive | Named 0, weak 4; camera |
| Pillar marks, matched to their stories | All four, by every rater; Explain at low to medium | All four, by every rater; Explain and Track at low |

At 16 px, raters found several Overprint icons fall apart (Explain, Testify, Act, Ballot and Campaign money among them). Moving part holds up better at 16 px.

## Family judgment

Every rater asked chose Overprint as the more finished family and the one they would rather see on a civic news website: three of three here, and the stage 2 rater before them. One said Moving part "holds up better at 16 px."

## What it says

- On style, Overprint leads clearly.
- On subjects, the two concepts share the same weak spots: Ballot and Campaign money read as a printer or tray and as a camera in both. Those are drawing problems for the build-out, whichever concept wins.
- Testify is the one subject where Moving part reads better than Overprint.
- Overprint's 16 px cut needs another pass before it ships.
