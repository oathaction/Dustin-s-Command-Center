## Round 1's calls, now rules for every round 2 chart

Every maker applies these from the first draft, so the review does not find them again.

1. **The named mark (D3-477, D3-489, D3-490, D3-620).** The mark a title names takes the view's red point (Rule 05.3, Rule 14.6) through `labels.point`, which alone gives every other mark the light fill. Where a U.S. reader would tie the named mark to a party (a state, a Congress or chamber whose majority the reader knows, an election result, a policy tied to the sitting president), it takes the subject ink against the light fill through `labels.named`, with no red. Grade glosses in a note take Rule 13.3's key glosses word for word (the data lead's ruling 2).
4. **H09.** No chart, note or sentence splits a period at an inauguration date or gives counts on both sides of one (D3-619). A window is set by the data's reach, with the reason printed.
5. **Values with their names (D3-505, CD BM1).** A figure never sits alone on a line; a name's last word goes down with it. Row names sit flush left in every listing form (D3-584).
6. **Phone drawings (D3-487, D3-534, D3-554).** A spec whose form changes under 480 px says `phone: "differs"`; the guide then shows the 358 px phone drawing at a desk. A long figure's desk phone drawing may show its first rows only (`desk_phone`), saying so.

9. **Every figure in a title or note is checked by script against the data**, with the check named in `notes_internal`. Read every title from the left and the right.
