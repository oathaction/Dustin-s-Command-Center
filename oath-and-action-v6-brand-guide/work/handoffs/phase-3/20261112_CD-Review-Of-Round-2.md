## Required changes

### Must fix

**A-M1. S45's title claims a share its drawing cannot show.** Owners: maker A (title, alt, notes), editor (words), data lead (sign-off).
- Title: "Homes in buildings of five or more units made up 36 percent of the new homes finished in 2024, their largest share in any year from 2000 to 2025." The stacked columns draw counts in thousands; no share is drawn, and a reader cannot compare the five-or-more part's share across columns of different heights. Entry 14.50's own "Use instead" sends shares to Rule 14.51. A builder copying this specimen learns to title a stack with a claim it cannot show.
- Fix: title the count the stack shows, checked by script against `census-nrc-completions-by-structure.csv`, for example "Builders finished 591,700 homes in buildings of five or more units in 2024, more than in any other year from 2000 to 2025." (591.7 thousand against 467.7 in 2025 and 438.3 in 2023.) Keep the 36 percent and the 1974 record in a note.

**A-M2. S47's red covers far more than 5 percent of a view.** Owners: data lead (ruling), editor (Rule 14.6 and entry 14.52), engineer (a kit check), maker A (S47).
- Rule 05.1: "Pillar Red stays within 5 percent" of every view, and chapter 05 calls each screen, card, slide or printed page a view. Measured by pixel count: S47's red tile is 19.7 percent of its 1440 px PNG export and 18.4 percent of its 390 px export, and 10.6 percent of a 390 by 844 px phone screen in the guide. A treemap's named tile is usually its largest, so entry 14.52's rule ("The part the title names takes the red point") will break Rule 05.1 nearly every time.
- Fix: the data lead rules an area case for Rule 14.6. Where the named mark's area would pass 5 percent of the view, it takes the subject ink with its words on the ground's plate, and the view spends no red. The engineer adds a check that measures Pillar Red's share of each export and fails a spec over 5 percent. Maker A redraws S47 under the ruling and sets its other tiles in the track gray, so the drawing reads as Newsprint with one dark tile rather than a gray slab. The editor states the case once, in Rule 14.6, and changes entry 14.52's sentence to cite it.

**B-M1. S58's band does not read as a band.** Owners: maker B (S58), engineer (`K.parts.band`), data lead (sign-off).
- Stone magnitude step 1 on Newsprint measures 1.16 to 1 by the WCAG formula (sampled at 1x from the guide). At 1440 px the band is about 11 px tall round a 2 px line and reads as the line's shadow, on Charcoal as on Newsprint; on a phone it nearly disappears. The entry is "Draw a band around a line to show each year's margin of error", and the band is the one thing it teaches. Its edge also fails 3 to 1 for a graphic the reader needs.
- Fix: keep the step 1 fill and draw each edge of the band as a 1 px line in the context ink, which passes 3 to 1 on both grounds, through `K.parts.band` so S59's outer band takes the same edge. Then look at S58 at 1x at 1440 and 390 px on both grounds: the band should read before the reader finds the label "±$1,040".

## Chapter 14 as a reference, and its weight

- **It still reads as a reference a designer would trust.** Every entry follows one order (rule, use it when, use instead, why it works, the specimen, the reader's question, builders), every specimen carries the evidence chip, sentence title, measure line, source line and live table, and the local contents groups 96 rules under 10 built families. The weakness is length: round 2's entries run 1,409 to 5,961 px tall at 1440 px, median 2,410, so 82 of them make a long scroll. Round 5's selection guide should show every form as a small drawing by question.
- **Weight holds.** Round 2's 26 specimen entries average 12,368 bytes (median 11,618) in the build against a share of 15,872 bytes for each of the 36 still to come, so the share rose by 1,968 bytes this round. Map geometry is held apart at 250,000 bytes and the sketch entries at 85,000. The page projects to 15,042,488 of 15,500,000 bytes. Rounds 3 and 4 have room to reach the bar if each plate keeps its data to the rows it draws; A-M2's check and B-M1's edges cost a few hundred bytes.
