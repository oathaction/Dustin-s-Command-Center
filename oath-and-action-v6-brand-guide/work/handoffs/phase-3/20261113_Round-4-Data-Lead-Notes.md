### How the test runs this round

Round 2's five principles and round 3's three additions hold unchanged (round 3 notes, "How the test runs this round"), with RF-1, RF-2, RF-17 and the brief's calls 1 to 8. Two additions for round 4's families:

1. **A set of facts, fields or parts takes no named mark.** A by-the-numbers panel, comparison cards, an annotated page and a calendar answer with every item they hold, as S60's indicator row and S71's grid do. Red appears nowhere on them, and no item takes the subject ink to stand above the others.
2. **Words that are text stay text.** Under the brief's call 8 and R4-3, a figure, a field, a step's words and a callout are HTML text in the text ink. Ink rulings below apply to the marks that carry data or order (a unit mark, a circle, a path, a day mark, a dot), which stay in SVG.

**No red point in round 4.** No title names one point among many: the icon array and the circles are areas (RF-1), the diagrams answer with their path (round 3, principle 1), the sets with every item (principle 1 above), and the social card keeps S19's two named states in the subject ink. Pillar Red therefore appears on no round 4 plate. That is the rule applied, not a choice for balance.
