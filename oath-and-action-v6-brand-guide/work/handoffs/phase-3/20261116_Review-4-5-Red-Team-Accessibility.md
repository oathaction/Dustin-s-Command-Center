## 5. Measured reach

Measured first by script on the markup of the copy (`scripts/reach.py` in the scratch folder), then in Chromium where marked. Figures in this section are from the markup unless a line says "in Chromium".

**(a) The masthead covering a returned focus (2.4.11 met, 2.4.12 not), shell-wide.** From the markup: six CSS rules read `--head-h` (the `scroll-margin-top` on `main, main *, .colophon *`; `.alias`; the rail's sticky top and height; `.cover`'s height; `.route-head`; the chapter's local contents). The page holds 2,663 ids in `main`, 3,748 in-page links (3,726 of them land in `main`), 2,233 links that leave a way back (`a.xref` or `a[data-oa-xref]`) and 147 places a way back returns to (`[data-return-label][id]`).

In Chromium (16:50 to 16:55 UTC, `scripts/dr278.cjs`, default text): the masthead measures 101 px against a `--head-h` of 64 px at every width tested from 1024 to 1280 px, not only to 1100 (1024, 1040, 1056, 1072, 1088, 1100, 1104, 1120, 1136, 1152, 1200, 1280); it fits at 1366 and 1440 (65 px against 64, its 1 px rule). At 360 px it measures 133 px against 88: the menu button drops to a second line once the 24 px wordmark returns at 22.5em, so a phone at 360 px loses 45 px. 320, 390, 768 and 1000 px fit (89 against 88).

I jumped to all 581 landings a hash or a way back can make in `main` (369 rules, 65 groups, 147 return places). At 1024 and at 1100 px, 212 of the 581 land with their top under the masthead, about 17 px of each, none wholly: every group (65 of 65) and every return place (147 of 147, the selection guide's 127 forms and "Where to start"'s 20 steps among them). The 369 rules clear, because their headings sit lower in their blocks. At 1440 px none is covered.

The fix, measured: set `--head-h` from the masthead's own height with a `ResizeObserver` in `guide-shell.js` (CSS keeps today's values for scripts off). With the masthead's full height: 0 of 581 covered at 1024 and 1100, all 581 landings 37 px lower there, and every landing at 1440 1 px lower because the full height counts the masthead's 1 px rule. Using `clientHeight`, which leaves the rule out, is measured in the second pass below. What else moves with it: the rail's sticky top and height, the local contents' sticky top, `.route-head`, `.alias` and `.cover`, by the same amount at the same widths. Owner: component engineer (the shell).

Second pass in Chromium (17:03 to 17:08 UTC, `scripts/dr278c.cjs`, every 2 px from 300 to 420 and every 4 px from 700 to 1440):

**(b) The words inside every `role="img"` stage.** From the markup: 735 plate stages, 416 of them `role="img"` and 319 `role="group"`. 413 of the 416 image stages hold HTML words, 5,008 runs of text in all, in 19 chapters (purpose 41, voice 51, identity 19, ampersand 18, color 15, type 18, layout 12, lockmark 7, pillar icons 10, symbols 15, icons 31, graphic devices 16, evidence 11, data visualization 15, systems graphics 25, motion and states 19, components 49, accessibility 15, tokens 22, outside a chapter 4). In Chromium (16:55 to 17:12 UTC, `scripts/dr278b.cjs`, `scripts/geo.cjs`): the words are exposed today. Under Rule 11.10's tracker, whose name is its 1,308-character alt, Chromium's tree holds 33 text nodes (the heading, each measure's title and status line, the key; round 5 counted 106 nodes of every kind); 14.144's failure plate holds 36 and 18.20's anatomy 61. **(c) "Where to start" at 320 px with text at 200 percent.** From the markup: 20 `span.step-to` lines, all on the start page's routes, each inside `a.xref.step-go`. `.step-to` is `display: inline-block` with no `overflow-wrap`, and its words hold no-break spaces from the widow rule ("Components and\u00a0interaction", "Status and utility\u00a0icons", "Layout and\u00a0spacing", "choose\u00a0them"), so an inline block's narrowest width is its longest unbroken run.

In Chromium (16:58 UTC, root text at 200 percent as the guide check sets it): at 320 px two lines pass the window, "Appendix · Identity proofs" (right edge 324 px) and "Chapter 18 · Components and interaction" (330 px); at 360 and 390 px none does. The measured fix, `.step-to { overflow-wrap: anywhere }`, brings both inside, and at the default text size it moves none of the 20 lines at 320, 390, 768, 1024 or 1440 px. **The page still scrolls sideways by 33 px at 320 px with 200 percent text after the fix** (17:12 UTC, `scripts/wide.cjs`, which lists the outermost element past the window that no scrolling frame holds). The cause is chapter 26's four live code sets, each drawn light and dark: `c-26-download` and `c-26-warning` (right edges 353 px) and `c-26-caption` and `c-26-chart` (322 px), eight `div.plate-stage.code-stage` in all. So the fix the brief names touches 20 lines on one page and moves nothing at the default size, and a second fix, letting a code stage's preview shrink to its frame or scroll inside it, is needed before 320 px at 200 percent stops scrolling. WCAG asks for 320 px at 100 percent (1.4.10) and 200 percent at some width (1.4.4), both met, so both are low. Owners: component engineer (the start page and the code stage CSS), engineer (chapter 26's code sets).

**(d) Chapter 26's token table script.** From the markup: one element on the whole page carries the class `tt`, the token table's own (`s-26-token-table`, chapter 26).
