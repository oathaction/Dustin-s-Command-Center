From our systems atlas, Loop R1

“Wealth and political influence reinforce each other”

Our atlas grades this loop plausible: its middle step is the least certain, and it runs only on issues where large donors and middle-income voters want different things.

Ready. Start at wealth concentration, and suppose it rises. Press Play, or Next step to follow one connection at a time.

  1. Wealth concentration raises money in politics. People with more wealth can give more to campaigns, outside groups and lobbyists, so wealth concentrated at the top raises the money spent to influence politics.
  2. Money in politics can raise policy tilt to insiders. Donors gain meetings with officeholders and a say in which issues reach the agenda and how bills are drafted, which can shift rules toward their interests. Our atlas grades this step plausible, the least certain in the loop.
  3. Policy tilt to insiders raises wealth concentration, where the loop began. Tax breaks for investment income and rules that protect existing profits deliver most of their value to the top tenth of households, which adds to their wealth.
