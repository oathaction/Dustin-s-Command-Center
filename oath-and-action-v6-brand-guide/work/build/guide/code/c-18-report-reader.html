How a county pays for its roads, page 1

Where the money comes from

The county pays for its roads from its share of the state’s fuel tax, from property taxes and from a yearly vehicle fee.

How a county pays for its roads, page 2

What a mile of road costs

Repaving a mile of two-lane road costs more each year as the price of asphalt rises, so the county plans its repairs three years ahead.

How a county pays for its roads, page 3

Repairs that wait

A road that waits too long for a new surface wears down to its base, and the county then has to rebuild it from the ground up.

How a county pays for its roads, page 4

Who decides the list

The county engineer ranks every road by its condition, and the county board votes on the list each spring at a public meeting.
