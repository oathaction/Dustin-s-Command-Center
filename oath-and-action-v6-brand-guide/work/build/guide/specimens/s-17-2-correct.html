Six durations, drawn to one scale

  • Fast, 120 ms--oa-motion-duration-fast

    Hover and toggles.

  • Standard, 200 ms--oa-motion-duration-standard

    Reveals, panels, a mark that lands and a line that draws on its own.

  • Slow, 320 ms--oa-motion-duration-slow

    A move across a page or a component, such as the ring that slides to a bill’s next step.

  • Stagger, 40 ms--oa-motion-duration-stagger

    The wait between marks that move one after another.

  • Hinge, 680 ms--oa-motion-duration-hinge

    The hinge, played on request only.

  • Lever, 560 ms--oa-motion-duration-lever

    The lever landing, played on request only.

Under reduced motion each of the six durations is 0 ms (Rule 17.4).

Three easings, each drawn as its curve

Time runs left to right and progress runs bottom to top, from where a mark starts to where it rests.

  • Standard

    cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)

    --oa-motion-easing-standard

    A mark that moves from one place to another.

  • Enter

    cubic-bezier(0, 0, 0.2, 1)

    --oa-motion-easing-enter

    A mark that arrives: a panel, a land, a line that draws.

  • Exit

    cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)

    --oa-motion-easing-exit

    A mark that leaves, at the fast duration.
