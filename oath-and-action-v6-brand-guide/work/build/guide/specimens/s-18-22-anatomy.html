Wealth and political influence reinforce each other

Step 3 of 3: Policy tilt to insiders raises wealth concentration. Tax breaks for investment income and rules that protect existing profits deliver most of their value to the top tenth of households, which adds to their wealth.1

  1. Wealth concentration raises money in politics. People with more wealth can give more to campaigns, outside groups and lobbyists, so wealth concentrated at the top raises the money spent to influence politics.
  2. Money in politics can raise policy tilt to insiders. Donors gain meetings with officeholders and a say in which issues reach the agenda and how bills are drafted, which can shift rules toward their interests. Our atlas grades this step plausible, the least certain in the loop.
