Locked lines
Our four brand lines whose wording never changes: the tagline, the mission, the reader promise and the editorial commitment. Rule 02.23
Pillars
Explain, Track, Connect and Act, the four jobs our work does, always in that order. Each pillar has an icon, set with its word beside it.
The seven colors
Newsprint, a warm off-white, is our page, and Charcoal carries the words. Pillar Red marks one thing in each view. Prussian, our dark blue, Stone, a mid gray, Verdigris, a muted green, and White support the rest. Chapter 05
View
One screen, card, slide or printed page. Rule 04.2
Working red
The one red a view may spend, on an error the reader must fix, the step they can take now or the point the headline names, in that order. Rule 05.3
Signature red
The red inside our marks and icons, such as the wordmark’s ampersand and the lockmark’s lever. It never counts as a view’s working red. Rule 05.3
Token
A named color, size or type setting in our code, such as --oa-color-text-primary-on-newsprint. Builders use the name, and writers can skip the Tokens lines.
Kicker
The short line above a headline that says what kind of piece it is. Rule 01.21
Deck
The line under a headline that says what the piece covers.
Hinge headline
A headline that joins a rule to what it produces with our ampersand, as in “The zoning code & the rent.” Rule 02.27
Hinge divider
A hairline broken by our ampersand, set where a piece turns from how a decision is made to what you can do. Rule 04.4
Lockup
Our name and marks set together in one fixed arrangement. Chapter 03
Our systems atlas
Who Do They Answer To?, our map of who makes public decisions and what shapes them. A loop in it is a chain of causes that returns to where it started, and its ten domains sort every issue by subject, such as elections and representation.
Evidence grade
How sure a claim is: documented, well supported, plausible, contested or hypothesis. House rule H08
Illustrative
Our label for anything invented for an example, such as a person, a quote, a place or a figure. House rule H03
