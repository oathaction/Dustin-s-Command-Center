StepWhat happensWhere the law sets it
1 FiledOne side files a complaint in a federal district court, the trial court for a case under federal law.Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, Rule 3; 28 U.S.C. 1331
2 Trial court rulingMotions, the exchange of evidence and, unless the two sides settle, a trial. The court enters its judgment, with or without a trial.Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, Rules 41, 56 and 58
3 AppealThe side that lost files a notice of appeal within 30 days of the judgment, or 60 days when the United States, a federal agency or a federal officer is a party.28 U.S.C. 1291 and 2107; Federal Rules of Appellate Procedure, Rule 4
4 Appeals court rulingA panel of three judges decides. It can affirm, reverse, vacate or modify the judgment, and send the case back to the trial court.28 U.S.C. 2106; Federal Rules of Appellate Procedure, Rules 40 and 41
5 Supreme Court petitionThe side that lost asks the Supreme Court to hear the case, within 90 days. The Court chooses: review is “not a matter of right, but of judicial discretion.”Rules of the Supreme Court, Rules 10, 13 and 16; 28 U.S.C. 1254 and 2101
6 Supreme Court rulingBriefs, argument and the Court’s decision, with the same powers as the court of appeals. A tie vote leaves the ruling below in place.28 U.S.C. 2106; Rules of the Supreme Court, Rule 16
