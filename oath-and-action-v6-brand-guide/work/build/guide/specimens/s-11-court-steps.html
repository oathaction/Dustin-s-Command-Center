StepWhat happens, and the law that sets it
1 Filed

One side sues the other in a federal district court, the trial court.

Law: Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, Rule 3; 28 U.S.C. 1331

2 Trial court ruling

The trial court decides, with or without a trial, unless the case settles or is dismissed.

Law: Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, Rules 41, 56 and 58

3 Appeal

The side that lost asks a court of appeals to review the ruling.

Law: 28 U.S.C. 1291 and 2107; Federal Rules of Appellate Procedure, Rule 4

4 Appeals court ruling

Three judges of the court of appeals decide.

Law: 28 U.S.C. 2106; Federal Rules of Appellate Procedure, Rules 40 and 41

5 Supreme Court petition

The side that lost asks the Supreme Court to hear the case, and the Court agrees or declines.

Law: Rules of the Supreme Court, Rules 10, 13 and 16; 28 U.S.C. 1254 and 2101

6 Supreme Court ruling

The Supreme Court decides.

Law: 28 U.S.C. 2106; Rules of the Supreme Court, Rule 16
