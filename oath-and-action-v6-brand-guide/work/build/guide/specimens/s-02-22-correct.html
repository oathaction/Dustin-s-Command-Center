H08’s evidence grades and the verb each one takes

GradeWhat it meansTitle verb
DocumentedAn event or fact on the public record, such as a statute or an official statistic.Record language, such as “passed” or “stood at”.
Well supportedSeveral credible studies, built to rule out other explanations, agree about direction.A direct finding, such as “raised” or “lowered”.
PlausibleA coherent mechanism with some supporting evidence, from studies that are weak or few.“Can” or “may”.
ContestedCredible studies disagree.Both readings, at the same length.
HypothesisA proposed relationship that needs stronger evidence.A label that marks it as untested.
