The grade line code

How sure the evidence is, drawn in the line itself, separate from color: solid for documented and well supported, dashed for plausible, dash-dot for contested, dotted for a hypothesis.

On Newsprint, at 2 and 3 px

  • Documented, on the public record
  • Well supported, strong research evidence
  • Plausible, some evidence
  • Contested, evidence disputed
  • Hypothesis, not yet tested

On Charcoal, at 2 and 3 px

  • Documented, on the public record
  • Well supported, strong research evidence
  • Plausible, some evidence
  • Contested, evidence disputed
  • Hypothesis, not yet tested
  • Tokens oa.chart.grade.documented, oa.chart.grade.wellSupported, oa.chart.grade.plausible, oa.chart.grade.contested, oa.chart.grade.hypothesis
  • Patterns Plausible, 4 and 2 for each pixel of the line’s width, square ends; Contested, 4 and 1.5 and 1 and 1.5 for each pixel of the line’s width, square ends; Hypothesis, 0 and 2.5 for each pixel of the line’s width, round ends; each pattern stretches so the line starts and ends on ink
  • Record square 7 px, before Documented, oa.chart.grade.recordSquare
  • On Prussian No chart draws on it. A chart in a Prussian panel sits on its own Newsprint or Charcoal plate, since the four group inks measure 1.00 to 2.79 to 1 on Prussian, under the 3 to 1 a mark needs, and red never sits on Prussian (Rule 05.3).
  • Contrast WCAG 2.2’s ratio, rounded down to two places, so no value rounds up across a bar: 4.5 to 1 for text and 3 to 1 for a mark.
  • Lightness OKLab’s, from 0 for black to 1 for white.
  • Gray Each swatch through the browser’s grayscale filter.
  • Color vision Protanopia (no red-sensing cones), deuteranopia (no green-sensing cones) and tritanopia (no blue-sensing cones), simulated by Machado, Oliveira and Fernandes (2009) at full strength, which models full color blindness only; a color distance is the OKLab distance between two colors.
