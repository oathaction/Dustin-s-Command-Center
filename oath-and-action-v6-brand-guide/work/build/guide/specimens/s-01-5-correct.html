Citizens in 23 states and DC have a working way to petition a proposed law or amendment onto the ballot.

Statewide citizen-initiated measures, by state, as read November 6, 2026

  • A working citizen initiative: 23 states and DC
  • In the constitution, unusable since a 2021 court ruling: Mississippi
  • No citizen initiative: 26 states

Source: Ballotpedia, States with initiative or referendum, 50 states and DC. Retrieved November 6, 2026. Mississippi’s process exists on paper but cannot be used after a 2021 state supreme court ruling.
