1. Every chapter rendered

    What it shows
    Each chapter draws whole at the widths readers use
    Method
    Screenshots at 1440, 1024, 390 and 360 px, looked at by a person
    Criteria
    1.4.10

  2. Report pages

    What it shows
    Every page of a report reads in the reader
    Method
    A screenshot of each page in the report reader
    Criteria
    1.4.10, 4.1.2

  3. Keyboard and focus

    What it shows
    Every control is reached in order, shows its ring and holds no trap
    Method
    A script presses Tab through the page and walks each component’s keyboard path; a person checks the order and the ring
    Criteria
    2.1.1, 2.1.2, 2.4.3, 2.4.7, 2.4.11

  4. Reading order and headings

    What it shows
    The outline makes sense read aloud
    Method
    The accessibility tree’s outline, read by script and by a person
    Criteria
    1.3.1, 1.3.2, 2.4.6

  5. Automated rules

    What it shows
    The faults a machine can find are gone
    Method
    axe-core with the WCAG 2.2 A and AA tags on every chapter
    Criteria
    Many, in part

  6. Contrast

    What it shows
    Text, marks, edges and focus rings stand out from their ground
    Method
    A script reads each rendered color pair, states included
    Criteria
    1.4.3, 1.4.11

  7. Text at 200 percent and 320 px

    What it shows
    Nothing is cut off or lost when text grows or the screen narrows
    Method
    Screenshots, and a script that finds sideways scroll and clipped words
    Criteria
    1.4.4, 1.4.10, 1.4.12

  8. Reduced motion

    What it shows
    Nothing moves on its own, and every motion has a still end
    Method
    The browser set to reduce motion, then each moving part played
    Criteria
    2.2.2, 2.3.1

  9. Forced colors

    What it shows
    Every mark, edge and state survives a contrast theme
    Method
    The browser set to forced colors, light and dark, then each part read by script and by eye
    Criteria
    1.4.1, 1.4.11

  10. Dark surfaces and long labels

    What it shows
    Components read on Charcoal and Prussian, with the longest real words
    Method
    Specimens on each surface, and labels from the atlas’s longest variable and reform titles
    Criteria
    1.4.3, 1.4.10

  11. Print

    What it shows
    A printed page keeps its words and order
    Method
    Print emulation and the print stylesheet
    Criteria
    1.3.2

  12. Downloads

    What it shows
    Each file arrives whole and named
    Method
    Press each download, open each file, check SVG validity and PNG size
    Criteria
    2.4.4

  13. Diagram and chart equivalents

    What it shows
    A reader who cannot see a drawing gets its connections
    Method
    Each drawing checked for its words or table and its reading guide
    Criteria
    1.1.1, 1.3.1

  14. Grayscale

    What it shows
    Nothing rests on color alone
    Method
    Grayscale screenshots of every data and systems specimen
    Criteria
    1.4.1
