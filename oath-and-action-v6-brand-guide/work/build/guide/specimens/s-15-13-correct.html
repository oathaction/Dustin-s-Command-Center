Loop R1“Wealth and political influence reinforce each other”

More wealth concentration, more money in politics, more policy tilt to insiders, and back to more wealth concentration.

Each step, and the weakest

  • 1 Connection L01 “Concentrated wealth funds more political spending”. Well supported, strong research evidence.
  • 2 Connection L02 “Political spending can tilt policy toward donors”. Plausible, some evidence. The atlas names it the weakest step.
  • 3 Connection L03 “Policy advantages add to wealth at the top”. Well supported, strong research evidence.

In the atlas’s evidence summary, the middle step, Connection L02, “is plausible and the weakest.”

Reading a step

  • money, raises: money is what moves along the connection (Rule 15.4), and raises says the next variable moves the same way as the one before it (Rule 15.3).
  • Ticks near a line’s start count its delay, the time between a change and its effect: one for under 2 years, two for 2 to 10 years, three for over 10 years (Rule 13.5).
