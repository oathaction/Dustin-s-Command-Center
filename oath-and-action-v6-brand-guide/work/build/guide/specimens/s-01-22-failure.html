Alder County votes on backyard cottages March 9. Send a comment by March 3 or speak at the meeting.

The Board of Supervisors will decide whether homeowners may build one small cottage behind a house, which the zoning code calls an accessory dwelling unit.

Who decides

Five county supervisors decide, and three votes pass the change.

Why it matters

More homes would ease rents across the county, and the change costs taxpayers nothing.

What you can do

Send a written comment by 5 p.m. Wednesday, March 3, 2027.

Tell the supervisors to vote yes
