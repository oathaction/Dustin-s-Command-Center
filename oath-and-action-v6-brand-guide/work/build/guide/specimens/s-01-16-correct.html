Does pay rise with productivity?

Credible studies disagree. Two readings, at the same length.

One reading

Economists at Harvard University find that faster productivity growth has still brought faster pay growth for typical and average workers, in the authors’ estimates.

The other reading

Economists at the Economic Policy Institute find that typical workers’ pay has grown far more slowly than productivity since 1979, in the authors’ measures.

Sources: Anna M. Stansbury and Lawrence H. Summers, Harvard University, “Productivity and Pay: Is the Link Broken?”, National Bureau of Economic Research Working Paper 24165, December 2017, 1973 to 2016, United States; Economic Policy Institute, “The Productivity-Pay Gap”, 1979 to 2026, United States. Retrieved November 6, 2026. The two differ in the price index they use and in how they count benefits; our systems atlas grades the connection plausible and contested.
