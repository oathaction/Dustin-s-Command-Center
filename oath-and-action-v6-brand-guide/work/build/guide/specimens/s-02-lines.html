Retired, kept as a record

Our fifth edition’s six supporting lines retire with no role. Each fails a writing rule below, and the lines above already do their jobs.

  • “Know the rules. Find the lever.”
  • “Institutions endure when people act.”
  • “The fine print affects the big picture.”
  • “Bring your questions. They belong here.”
  • “Here is what the record shows. …”
  • “The rules connect. The gaps create opportunity. …”

Our copy of that edition keeps only the openings of the last two.
