StepWhat happens
1 FiledA legislator files the bill, and it goes to a committee.
2 HearingThe committee holds a hearing and takes testimony on the bill. Many state legislatures take testimony from the public there. In Congress, the committee hears the witnesses it invites. A bill’s own page says how to take part.
3 Committee voteThe committee votes on whether to send the bill to the full House or Senate.
4 First chamberThe full House or Senate, wherever the bill was filed, votes on it.
5 Second chamberThe other chamber takes the bill up. Its own committee can hold a hearing first, and then the full chamber votes and both chambers agree on one text. Nebraska has one chamber, so a Nebraska bill keeps this step under a slash.
6 Governor or PresidentThe governor signs a state bill and the President signs a federal one. Either can veto it, and lawmakers can vote to override a veto.
