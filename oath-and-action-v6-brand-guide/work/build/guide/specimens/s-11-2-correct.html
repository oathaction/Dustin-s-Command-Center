Each mark is drawn on step 4, at true size in a 26 px circle.

  • Done

    The step happened.

  • Now

    The bill is at this step, and the words beside the tracker name it.

  • You can take part now

    The bill is at this step, and the public can act before a date. The red disc, its place on the ring and the words carry it together.

  • Not yet

    The bill has not reached this step.

  • Never happened

    The bill moved past this step without it, as when a committee votes without holding a hearing. It keeps its number, so the count still runs to six.

  • Stopped

    The bill ended here, as when a committee votes it down or a veto stands. The line ends at this step.
