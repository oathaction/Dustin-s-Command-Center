Chapter 11

Status and utility icons

Give each record state or interface action a glyph and a word that read without color.

In this chapter

    • 11.1 Follow every bill through six numbered steps with plain names.
    • 11.2 Draw each step’s state as a shape, and say it in words.
    • 11.3 Say in words when a bill becomes law or stops.
    • 11.4 Mark the one way to take part in red, on its own shape.
    • 11.5 Use numbered steps in lists and on story cards.
    • 11.6 Turn the same steps upright on a bill’s own page.
    • 11.7 Size the marks for reading, and the targets for a thumb.
    • 11.8 Follow a records request through six steps, and end it in plain words.
    • 11.9 Follow an agency rule through six steps.
    • 11.10 Follow a ballot measure through six steps, and wait for the certified count.
    • 11.11 Follow a court case through six steps, and end a denied petition at step 5.
    • 11.12 Say a wait or a later step in words, and keep the count at six.
    • 11.13 Set each utility icon beside its word.
    • 11.14 Give each drawing one meaning.
    • 11.15 Mark a link to another site, and open it in the same tab.
    • 11.16 Keep topic and atlas symbols out of buttons.
    • 11.17 Give each error, success and warning its mark and its words.
    • 11.18 Give an error the red when it meets the other red jobs.
