The school board votes on the new bell schedule April 14.

The proposal moves the high school’s first bell from 7:25 to 8:10 a.m. Anyone may speak for two minutes before the vote.

Buses would reach each stop about 40 minutes later. The middle and elementary schools keep their hours.

The agenda and the full proposal are on the district’s website.
