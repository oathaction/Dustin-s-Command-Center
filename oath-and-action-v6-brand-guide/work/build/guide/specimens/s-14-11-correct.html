Median household income stood at $87,460 in 2025, in 2025 dollars

Median household income in 2025 dollars, each year from 1967, the first in the Census Bureau’s series, to 2025, United States

The line breaks twice where the survey changed. In 2013 it asked new income questions, and in 2017 it moved to a new processing system; each year prints both its estimates, the old method’s as an open dot and the new one’s filled.

The 2025 figure carries a margin of error of ±$1,040 at 90 percent confidence.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, Income in the United States: 2025, Table 2, median household income, 1967 to 2025, United States. Retrieved November 11, 2026. Survey estimates in 2025 dollars with 90 percent margins of error; methods changed in several years, marked in the table.
