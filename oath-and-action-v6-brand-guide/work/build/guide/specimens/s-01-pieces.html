Explainer·Utility rates

Why is my electric bill going up?

A regulator or a board approves your rate.

Who decides

For an investor-owned utility, your state’s public utility commission sets rates in a rate case, its formal review of the utility’s request. For public power and cooperatives, a city council, a utility board or a co-op board does.

What you can do

Find your utility’s name on your bill, then look up its open rate case on your state commission’s site for the dates to comment.

Alder County’s public works department schedules street repairs, and the Board of Supervisors sets its road budget each spring.

What to say in 90 seconds

Give the street and the date you reported the damage. Then ask the board when the repair is scheduled and what it will cost.
