The selected band is the ground of the current or selected row in a list, paired with the 4 px bar and bold words, and says which marks may sit inside it in the dark theme. The 24 px icon size names the 37 topic and atlas symbols on Rule 10.4's list. The ask fill says it is Newsprint on Prussian.

Each description now says what the chapters say and what the page draws: chapter 26's table prints them for readers, and tokens.json carries them to every product.
