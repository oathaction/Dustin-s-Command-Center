State senate committees should post every vote within two days.

What the record shows

Documented: 14 of 20 committees posted no roll calls last session. Source: the senate clerk’s journal, 2026. Illustrative

The strongest case against

Committee chairs in both parties say posting every procedural vote would discourage early compromise and need new clerk staff. Sources: committee testimony, February 2027; the clerk’s estimate. Illustrative
