Pillar Red on Newsprint measures 2.96:1, under the 3:1 guideline. Every red part is a drawn shape that the one-color editions carry in Charcoal or Newsprint, so color is never the only carrier of meaning; on Newsprint the red adds emphasis to a shape that is already there. A Newsprint tile on a White page has a soft edge (1.41:1); the tile shape is decoration there, not information.
