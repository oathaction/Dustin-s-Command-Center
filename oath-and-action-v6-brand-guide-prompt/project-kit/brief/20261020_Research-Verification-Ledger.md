# Research Verification Ledger: Oath & Action Brand Guide, Edition 06

## Method and an important limit

WebFetch did not work in this session. Every WebFetch call, on every domain tried (cooperhewitt.org, fieldworkfacility.com, awwwards.com, shopify.com, ncase.me, w3.org, ourworldindata.org, business.adobe.com, canva.com, climateinteractive.org, transformmagazine.net), failed with "getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND". A direct curl test to ncase.me was refused by the session's egress proxy (403, host not allowed). This is a sandbox network policy, not evidence that the sites are down.

So no page in this ledger was fetched. All verification comes from WebSearch, mostly run with `allowed_domains` limited to the official domain, so that what came back came only from that domain. Status labels mean:

- VERIFIED: seen in search results from the official domain (the award body, the agency, or the maker), and consistent across results.
- PARTLY: part of the claim was confirmed, or it was confirmed only by the maker's own page or a secondary outlet, or sources conflict.
- UNVERIFIED: not found on an official domain.
- CONTRADICTED: an official source says otherwise.

The "Reachable" column has a two-part answer. "Fetch: failed" means WebFetch could not load the page (true for every URL here). "Indexed: yes" means the exact URL showed up as a live result from the official domain in search, which is the best reachability proxy I had. Two domains could not be searched at all, because the search tool refused them: nytimes.com and economist.com (and opensecrets.org). Anything on those domains is marked UNVERIFIED.

Before the guide quotes exact wording, someone with normal web access should open each URL. This matters most for the two Part A items whose details came from summaries: the Awwwards award labels and the full Cooper Hewitt winner list.

### Corrections to the prompt

1. **Fieldwork Facility (A2).** The Gold is not for the identity. Corrected wording: "Fieldwork Facility's wayfinding for the V&A East Storehouse won Gold for Best wayfinding or signage at the Transform Awards Europe 2026 (ceremony 26 March 2026, London), credited jointly to the V&A and Fieldwork Facility; the studio's site records it as March 2026."
2. **Shopify (A3).** Awwwards titles the site "The Renaissance Edition"; it is not listed as "Winter '26 Edition". Corrected wording: "Shopify's Winter '26 Edition site, 'The Renaissance Edition' by Shopify Design (launched 10 December 2025), was named Awwwards Site of the Month for February 2026, after Awwwards Developer Award and Business & Services Honors in December 2025."
3. **Cooper Hewitt (A1).** Accurate as worded. For precision, add: "announced 25 February 2026; honored at the National Design Awards Gala on 19 May 2026." When describing Kurgan's work, use the museum's phrase "spatial computation, data visualization, and digital cartography," and cite Million Dollar Blocks to Columbia's Center for Spatial Research, not to the museum.
4. **Canva (A4b).** The report came out in December 2025, not 2026. Corrected wording: "Canva's 2026 Design Trends report, 'Imperfect by Design' (published December 2025), names ten trends." Its trend names are consumer-template styles. Do not present them as editorial or civic design norms.
5. **Adobe (A4a).** Accurate as worded. Add that it names four trends and is vendor marketing research. Its statistics come from studies Adobe cites (WGSN, The Drum).
6. **OWID (A5a).** This post is older (about 2023). Do not present it as a 2024 to 2026 reference. Corrected wording: "Our World in Data's Grapher redesign post (circa 2023) documents the chart, map and table tabs, SVG, PNG and CSV downloads, sources and full-screen conventions."
7. **LOOPY (A5b).** It dates from 2017. Label it "older, canonical".
8. **Information is Beautiful Awards (Part B).** The "Kantar" name is out of date. The awards now run under the Data Visualization Society. The most recent edition is the one labeled "2024" on the awards site, presented in June 2025. DVS says the awards are on hiatus in 2026 and will resume in 2027.
9. **Malofiej 2026 (Part B).** I found no 2025 or 2026 edition. Wikipedia says the organizers paused the awards on 1 October 2021. I could not confirm this on the official site, so do not cite a "Malofiej 2026 Best of Show".
10. **Brand New Awards (Part B).** Discontinued. UnderConsideration posted "Brand New Awards: No More" on 7 January 2015. There is no "most recent" edition to cite.
